# LILITH PROJECT MASTER INSTRUCTION

This project is for creating, refining, and maintaining artwork of Lilith, a mascot-style chibi demoness character.

All Lilith artwork, references, emotes, pose sheets, expression sheets, cookbook illustrations, icons, promotional art, and related visual material must follow the project’s canonical source documents.

The purpose of these instructions is to prevent design drift and ensure every future version of Lilith feels like the same character.

---

# Source Priority Order

When creating or evaluating Lilith artwork, use the following source priority:

1. Lilith Face Reference
2. Lilith Character Reference
3. Project Master Instruction
4. Canon Character Guide
5. Character Requirements
6. Canon Color Palette
7. Style Guide
8. Body Proportion Guide
9. Expression Bible
10. Pose Bible
11. Variant-specific guides
12. Design Rules / Drift Prevention
13. The current prompt

Higher-priority sources override lower-priority sources.

The current prompt may request a pose, expression, prop, outfit variant, format, or composition, but it must not override Lilith’s core identity unless explicitly stated by the user.

The Face Reference is the highest authority for Lilith’s identity.

---

# Character Identity

Lilith is a mascot-style chibi demoness.

She should always read as:

- Sassy
- Mischievous
- Clever
- Confident
- Self-assured
- Playful villain
- Trickster
- Slightly amused

Lilith should not read as:

- Generic demoness
- Seductive succubus
- Grimdark villain
- Cruel monster
- Sad character
- Innocent cute girl
- Angry without reason
- Realistic fantasy woman

Her defining personality trait is mischievous amusement.

She should look like she knows something the viewer does not.

---

# Core Canon Features

Lilith has two layers of canon features: identity-critical traits and design-critical supporting traits.

Identity-critical traits are the features that make Lilith immediately read as Lilith. These must be preserved across all canon artwork:

- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Black hair
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp mischievous eyes
- Slight smug expression
- Pointed ears
- Black horns
- Mischievous amusement

Design-critical supporting traits should be preserved whenever visible or relevant to the artwork:

- Black nails when hands are visible
- Gothic black outfit
- Gold jewelry or gold accents
- Bat wings when included
- Red spaded tail when included
- Compact mature-chibi mascot proportions
- Clean mascot-vector readability

If the artwork loses the black lips, visible fangs, heavy-lidded gold eyes, smug expression, or black sidebang silhouette, it is drifting away from canon Lilith.

Supporting traits should be corrected when visible, but the face identity always has the highest revision priority.

---

# Face Priority Rule

Lilith’s face carries most of her identity.

The face must remain recognizable before any other design feature.

Always preserve:

- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp gold eyes
- Confident gaze
- Black lips
- Small fangs
- Slight smugness
- Black sidebang
- Mischievous amusement

Do not allow expressions, poses, props, or style changes to erase her face identity.

Even when Lilith is surprised, laughing, confused, proud, annoyed, or performing an action, she should still feel like Lilith.

---

# Body Proportion Rule

Lilith uses compact mature-chibi mascot proportions.

Required:

- Large head
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Small torso
- Wide readable silhouette
- Simplified limbs
- No elongated body

Avoid:

- Tall redesigns
- Thin redesigns
- Long legs
- Long torso
- Fashion-doll proportions
- Realistic anatomy
- Hypersexualized anatomy

Removing wings, tail, props, or background elements must not make Lilith taller, thinner, or less chibi.

---

# Style Rule

Lilith should be drawn in a clean mascot-vector cartoon style.

Preferred:

- Thick outlines
- Clean silhouette
- Simple shapes
- Minimal detail
- Flat colors or simple cel shading
- Emote-friendly readability
- Sticker-like clarity
- Easy-to-redraw design

Avoid:

- Realism
- Painterly rendering
- Soft airbrush shading
- Complex textures
- Excessive detail
- Semi-realistic fantasy art
- Overly complex anime rendering

Lilith should remain readable at small sizes, including Discord emote size.

---

# Color Rule

Use the Canon Color Palette.

Preserve:

- Red skin
- Black hair
- Black horns
- Black clothing
- Black lips
- Black nails
- Gold eyes
- Gold jewelry
- Red tail
- Black and red wings

Avoid hue shifts, alternate palettes, or reinterpretations unless explicitly requested by the user.

Black lips, gold eyes, red skin, and black hair are especially important and should not be changed.

---

# Wings and Tail Rule

Canon Lilith includes bat wings and a red spaded tail unless the user specifically requests their removal.

When included:

- Wings should be bat-like, simple, black and red, and readable.
- Tail should be red, simple, curved, and have a spaded tip.

When removed:

- Only remove the wings and/or tail.
- Do not redesign the body.
- Do not change the face.
- Do not make her taller or thinner.
- Do not alter her personality or color identity.

A no-wing or no-tail Lilith is still canon Lilith, not a new character.

---

# Props, Poses, and Activities

Lilith may be shown in many poses and activities.

Allowed examples:

- Standing
- Presenting
- Explaining
- Laughing
- Cooking
- Reading
- Sneaking
- Running
- Strutting
- Holding items
- Reacting
- Posing for emotes

However:

- The face must remain visible.
- The expression must remain readable.
- The body proportions must remain compact.
- Props must not obscure her identity.
- The pose must not stretch or redesign her.
- The mischievous personality must remain visible.

The pose should serve Lilith. Lilith should not be redesigned to serve the pose.

---

# Background and Composition Rule

Lilith should remain the focal point.

Preferred:

- Simple background
- Transparent background
- Minimal decorative elements
- Clear readable composition
- Character-centered framing

Avoid:

- Busy scenery
- Cluttered props
- Backgrounds that compete with Lilith
- Effects that obscure the face
- Compositions where the character becomes secondary

For emotes, icons, and stickers, prioritize facial readability over scene complexity.

---

# Drift Prevention Rule

Reject or revise artwork if Lilith becomes:

- Too tall
- Too thin
- Too realistic
- Too detailed
- Too soft or innocent
- Too angry
- Too sad
- Too generic
- Too seductive
- Missing black lips
- Missing visible fangs
- Missing heavy-lidded gold eyes
- Missing black sidebang
- Missing black nails
- Wrong skin color
- Wrong eye color
- Wrong jewelry color
- Painterly instead of mascot-like

These are not minor issues. They weaken Lilith’s identity.

---

# Conflict Resolution

When there is a conflict:

- Face Reference overrides everything else.
- Character Reference overrides new artwork.
- Canon Character Guide overrides generic fantasy/demon assumptions.
- Character Requirements override stylistic experimentation.
- Canon Color Palette overrides lighting or palette variation.
- Style Guide overrides rendering complexity.
- Body Proportion Guide overrides general proportion assumptions.
- Design Rules / Drift Prevention functions as the review and enforcement layer for rejecting artwork that weakens recognition.
- Drift Prevention rules override prompt details that would weaken recognition.

When uncertain, preserve Lilith’s face, expression, palette, proportions, and mascot readability.

---

# Final Validation

Before approving any Lilith artwork, check:

1. Does she immediately read as Lilith?
2. Are the heavy-lidded gold eyes present?
3. Are the black lips clearly visible?
4. Are small fangs visible?
5. Is the black sidebang silhouette preserved?
6. Does she look mischievous, smug, or amused?
7. Is the body compact mature-chibi?
8. Are the canon colors preserved?
9. Is the style simple, clean, and emote-friendly?
10. Would the artwork still read at 128x128?
11. Would another artist be able to redraw her easily?
12. Did the artwork avoid generic demoness or succubus drift?

If any answer is no, revise the artwork.
