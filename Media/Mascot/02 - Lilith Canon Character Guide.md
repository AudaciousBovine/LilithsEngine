# LILITH - CANON CHARACTER GUIDE

## Purpose

This document defines Lilith's canonical character identity, personality, visual design, and design intent.

It exists to ensure every future piece of Lilith artwork is derived from the same character, whether the artwork is an emote, pose sheet, expression sheet, icon, cookbook illustration, reference sheet, promotional image, or mod-related artwork.

This file defines who Lilith is.

Other supporting files define more specific rules:

- Character Requirements define what features must be present.
- Canon Color Palette defines exact colors.
- Style Guide defines how Lilith should be rendered.
- Body Proportion Guide defines how Lilith's body should be shaped, scaled, and kept consistent.
- Design Rules / Drift Prevention defines what mistakes to reject.
- Expression Bible defines expression-specific rules.
- Pose Bible defines pose-specific rules.

When in doubt, preserve Lilith's face, personality, proportions, palette, and mascot readability.

# Character Summary

Lilith is a mascot-style chibi demoness with a mischievous, self-satisfied personality.

She is a playful villain, trickster, and gremlin queen type of character. She should feel clever, amused, and slightly smug, as if she is always one step ahead.

Her defining emotional impression is:

> She knows something you do not.

She should feel pleased with herself, not cruel or grim.

---

# Core Personality

Lilith should always read as:

- Sassy
- Mischievous
- Clever
- Confident
- Self-assured
- Playful villain
- Trickster
- Slightly amused
- Pleased with herself

Lilith should not read as:

- Generic demoness
- Seductive succubus
- Grimdark villain
- Cruel monster
- Sad character
- Depressed character
- Innocent cute girl
- Angry without reason
- Realistic fantasy woman
- Edgy horror demon

Lilith's mischief should feel playful, not malicious.

Her villainy should feel theatrical and amusing, not brutal or frightening.

---

# Face Identity

Lilith's face is the most important part of her design.

The following facial features are mandatory:

- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Black hair
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp mischievous eyes
- Slight smug expression
- Pointed ears
- Black horns

If the face loses the heavy-lidded gold eyes, black lips, small fangs, smugness, or black sidebang silhouette, it no longer reads strongly as Lilith.

---

# Body Proportions

Lilith uses compact mature-chibi mascot proportions.

"Mature-chibi" means she is not baby-like or toddler-like, but she still keeps mascot proportions: large head, compact body, short legs, simplified anatomy, and no realistic or fashion-doll body structure.

Required body traits:

- Large head relative to body
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Small torso
- Simplified limbs
- Wide, sturdy, readable silhouette
- Slightly narrower face, without becoming tall or thin

Avoid:

- Realistic proportions
- Tall redesigns
- Thin redesigns
- Long legs
- Long torso
- Fashion-doll proportions
- Hypersexualized anatomy
- Tiny head with realistic body ratio

Lilith should feel like a compact mascot character, not a realistic fantasy woman.

# Canon Outfit Identity

Lilith's outfit should read as gothic, black, simple, and mascot-friendly.

Core outfit identity:

- Black gothic dress or black gothic outfit
- Gold accents
- Gold jewelry
- Black stockings or dark legwear when visible
- Black boots or dark footwear when visible

The outfit supports Lilith's identity but should not compete with the face.

Avoid turning the outfit into unrelated modern clothing, complex fantasy armor, excessive lace detail, or a design that becomes more important than Lilith's expression.

---

# Wings and Tail

Canon Lilith includes bat wings and a red spaded tail unless a specific variant requests their removal.

Wings, when included:

- Bat-like
- Black outer structure
- Red inner membranes
- Simple readable shape
- Secondary to the face and body

Tail, when included:

- Red
- Simple curved shape
- Spaded tip
- Readable silhouette
- Used to add attitude without dominating the design

If wings or tail are removed by request, only remove those parts. Do not change Lilith's face, body proportions, palette, outfit identity, or personality.

---

# Hands and Nails

Lilith's hands should remain simplified and mascot-friendly.

Required when hands are visible:

- Five fingers when clearly shown
- Black nails
- Simple gesture-readable shapes

Hands should support attitude and pose. They should not become realistic, overly detailed, claw-like, or cover the face identity.

---

# Activity Rule

Lilith may be shown doing many activities, including reading, cooking, presenting, strutting, sneaking, running, laughing, reacting, holding items, or appearing in scene art.

No activity should redesign her.

Regardless of what Lilith is doing, preserve:

- Face identity
- Mischievous amusement
- Compact mature-chibi proportions
- Red-black-gold palette
- Simple mascot-vector readability

The activity should serve Lilith. Lilith should not be redesigned to serve the activity.

---

# Variant Rule

Variants are allowed when they preserve Lilith's identity.

A variant may change:

- Pose
- Expression
- Activity
- Prop
- Background
- Format
- Wing presence, if requested
- Tail presence, if requested
- Outfit theme, if explicitly requested

A variant must not change:

- Face identity
- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Black hair
- Black sidebang
- Black lips
- Small fangs
- Heavy-lidded mischievous gaze
- Compact chibi proportions
- Red-black-gold palette
- Mischievous personality read

No-wing or no-tail Lilith is still Lilith, not a new character design.

---

# Final Rule

Lilith is not defined by one accessory, one pose, or one outfit detail.

Lilith is defined by the combination of:

- Heavy-lidded gold eyes
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Red skin
- Black hair
- Compact chibi mascot proportions
- Mischievous amused personality
- Simple red-black-gold visual identity

Above all, Lilith should always look like she knows something you do not.
