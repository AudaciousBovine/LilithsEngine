# Lilith Canon Identity Rules

## Document Status

**Version:** 0.1 Draft  
**Canon Stage:** Draft Canon  
**Purpose:** Define Lilith’s core identity so future artwork, edits, reference sheets, emotes, style tests, and mascot assets remain consistent.

The current reference images are considered the primary draft source. They may still be altered before final approval, but all changes should be controlled, documented, and compared against the current design.

---

# 1. Core Character Identity

Lilith is a compact mature-chibi gothic demon mascot.

She has:

- Red skin
- Black hair
- Heavy-lidded sharp gold eyes
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Black curved horns
- Long pointed ears
- Black nails
- Gothic black clothing
- Gold jewelry
- Compact mascot proportions
- A smug, mischievous, self-assured personality

Lilith should feel like a clever little villainess, trickster, or gremlin queen who is amused by everything happening around her.

She should look like she knows something the viewer does not.

# 3. Personality Rules

Lilith’s personality should read as:

- Sassy
- Mischievous
- Clever
- Playfully villainous
- Confident
- Amused
- Self-assured
- A little smug
- Dramatic, but not cruel

Her default emotional state is not neutral innocence. Her default should feel like quiet amusement.

Even when she is angry, shocked, thinking, cooking, reading, or posing, she should still feel like the same clever gothic mascot.

## Avoid

Lilith should not read as:

- Innocent
- Generic cute chibi
- Childish
- Helpless
- Blank-faced
- Overly angry by default
- Monstrous
- Cruel
- Overly seductive
- Generic demon girl
- Generic anime mascot

---

# 4. Proportion Rules

Lilith uses **compact mature-chibi mascot proportions**.

## Required Proportion Traits

- Large head
- Short compact body
- Short torso
- Short arms
- Short legs
- Small readable hands
- Small boots
- Mature face structure
- Strong silhouette
- Compact body without becoming babyish

## Important Balance

Lilith should have a chibi-sized body, but not a childish face.

Her face should remain sharp, lidded, smug, and mature compared to normal chibi characters.

The rule is:

**Chibi body, mature face, gothic villain attitude.**

---

# 5. Face Rules

Lilith’s face is one of her most important identity anchors.

## Required Face Traits

- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp gold eyes
- Slightly narrowed gaze
- Angled or expressive brows
- Small sharp nose
- Black lips always visible
- Small fangs always visible
- Smug or amused expression language
- Slightly sly mouth shape

## Eye Rules

Lilith’s eyes should be:

- Gold
- Sharp
- Narrow
- Heavy-lidded
- Confident
- Slightly sleepy or unimpressed in shape, but still alert

Her eyes should not become huge, round, innocent, or overly sparkly.

## Mouth Rules

Lilith’s mouth must preserve:

- Black lips
- Clear lip shape
- Visible fangs
- Mischievous expression

Even in closed-mouth expressions, the black lips should be readable.  
Even in subtle expressions, at least small fangs should remain visible when possible.

## Nose Rules

Her nose should stay simple but sharp.

Avoid making it too round, too tiny, or too soft.

---

# 6. Hair Rules

Lilith’s hair is a major silhouette anchor.

## Required Hair Traits

- Black hair
- Large, fluffy, slightly messy hair mass
- Strong side-swept bang
- Hair partially frames or covers one side of the face
- Rough gothic painterly texture
- Dark highlights, not glossy shine

## Avoid

- Middle-parted hair unless specifically testing a variant
- Perfectly smooth hair
- Bright highlights
- Thin hair volume
- Symmetrical generic anime hair
- Removing the strong side-bang silhouette

---

# 7. Horn Rules

Lilith’s horns should remain recognizable and consistent.

## Required Horn Traits

- Black or charcoal horns
- Curved upward shape
- Positioned symmetrically on top of the head
- Textured with subtle ridges
- Strong silhouette
- Dark warm highlights

## Avoid

- Tiny horns
- Straight horns
- Bright red horns
- Smooth plastic-looking horns
- Horns that overpower the head
- Horns that disappear into the hair

---

# 8. Ear Rules

Lilith’s ears are long, pointed, and elf-like/demonic.

## Required Ear Traits

- Long pointed ears
- Red skin matching the body
- Clear silhouette outside the hair
- Slight dark inner-ear shading
- Sharp, clean lower edges

## Avoid

- Round ears
- Human ears
- Jagged or malformed ear edges
- Ears hidden completely by hair

---

# 9. Outfit Rules

Lilith wears a gothic black outfit with elegant villainess details.

## Required Outfit Traits

- Black gothic dress
- Off-shoulder neckline
- Dark lace or ruffle-like trim
- Jagged layered skirt shapes
- Dark sleeves or gloves
- Black boots
- Gold decorative accents
- Diamond-shaped jewelry motifs

The outfit should feel dark, ornate, and theatrical, but still simple enough to work as a mascot design.

## Avoid

- Bright clothing
- Modern casual clothing unless making a specific alternate outfit
- Overly complicated details that hurt readability
- Removing the gothic silhouette
- Making the outfit too soft, pastel, or princess-like

---

# 10. Jewelry and Accessory Rules

Gold jewelry is part of Lilith’s identity.

## Required Jewelry Traits

- Gold earrings
- Gold necklace or chest jewelry
- Diamond-shaped red/gold accents
- Gold belt or waist accents when visible
- Small gold boot or outfit details when appropriate

Jewelry should support the silhouette without overwhelming the character.

## Avoid

- Silver jewelry replacing gold
- Removing all jewelry
- Random accessory clutter
- Jewelry that looks modern instead of gothic/fantasy

---

# 11. Color Rules

Lilith’s palette should stay within a dark gothic red, black, and gold range.

## Core Palette Identity

- Skin: deep red
- Hair: black

- Eyes: bright gold
- Jewelry: warm gold
- Lips: black
- Nails: black
- Fangs: warm ivory
- Shadows: deep red and black
- Highlights: muted red, warm gold, and dark brown-black

# 12. Silhouette Rules

Lilith should be recognizable even as a black silhouette.

## Required Silhouette Anchors

- Large head
- Compact body
- Big side-swept hair mass
- Tall curved horns
- Long pointed ears
- Off-shoulder gothic dress shape
- Jagged skirt edges
- Small hands
- Short legs
- Boots
- Dangling diamond jewelry shapes

If these are lost, the design is drifting.

---

# 13. Expression Rules

Lilith should always emote with attitude.

## Core Expression Language

- Heavy eyelids
- Slight smirk
- Raised brow
- Sharp eyes
- Black lips
- Visible fangs
- Confident eye contact
- Playful villain energy

## Approved Expression Directions

Lilith can be:

- Neutral
- Smirking
- Mischievous
- Laughing
- Teasing
- Plotting
- Thinking
- Pouting
- Shocked in disbelief
- Angry
- Determined
- Proud
- Suspicious
- Amused

But every expression should still preserve her core personality.

## Avoid

- Blank expression
- Innocent wide-eyed look
- Crying helplessness as a default
- Fully hiding lips
- Fully hiding fangs
- Making her look generic or emotionally flat

---

# 14. Style Rules

Lilith’s default style is dark gothic comic-painterly mascot art.

## Required Style Traits

- Thick dark outlines
- Rough painterly texture
- Dramatic shadows
- Limited but strong highlights
- Dark red, black, and gold palette
- Slight grunge texture
- Strong silhouette readability
- Simplified forms with expressive detail

## Avoid

- Glossy 3D
- Smooth plastic shading
- Hyper-realism
- Soft pastel anime
- Overly clean corporate vector art
- Generic mobile-game chibi style
- Excessive background clutter

---

# 16. Editable Traits During Draft Phase

These traits may be tested before final canon is locked:

- Exact nose sharpness
- Exact lip size
- Exact fang size
- Eyelid heaviness
- Brow angle
- Horn thickness
- Horn texture
- Hair volume
- Skirt jaggedness
- Jewelry complexity
- Body compactness
- Texture intensity
- Background color
- Line roughness

Only one major trait should be tested at a time.

---

# 17. Design Drift Warnings

An image should be revised if Lilith starts to look:

- Too cute and innocent
- Too young
- Too generic
- Too human
- Too monstrous
- Too soft
- Too bright
- Too clean
- Too anime-idol-like
- Too different from the reference silhouette
- Missing black lips
- Missing fangs
- Missing gold eyes
- Missing gothic outfit identity

---

# 18. Canon Quality Checklist

Before accepting a Lilith image, check:

- Does she have red skin?
- Is her hair black?
- Are her eyes sharp, gold, and heavy-lidded?
- Are her black lips visible?
- Are her fangs visible?
- Are her horns black and curved?
- Are her ears long and pointed?
- Is her outfit gothic and black?
- Is her jewelry gold?
- Are her nails black?
- Is her body compact?
- Does her face look mature rather than childish?
- Does she look smug, clever, or mischievous?
- Is her silhouette still recognizable?
- Does she still feel like the same mascot?

If the answer to any major identity question is no, the image should not become canon.

---

# 19. Final Identity Summary

Lilith is a red-skinned, black-haired, gold-eyed gothic demon mascot with black lips, small fangs, curved horns, pointed ears, black nails, gothic black clothing, gold jewelry, and compact mature-chibi proportions.

She is mischievous, smug, clever, playful, and self-assured.

Her design should always balance three ideas:

**Dark gothic villainess**  
**Compact mascot readability**  
**Sassy mischievous charm**

When in doubt, preserve her face, silhouette, palette, and personality before changing anything else.
