# 1. Palette Identity Summary

Lilith's updated canon palette is now built from the uploaded turnaround reference:

**abyss black, smoked charcoal, warm black-brown highlights, dark oxblood red skin, rusty crimson edges, antique copper-gold jewelry, warm ivory fangs, black lips, black nails, and bright readable gold eyes.**

The overall read should be:

- darker
- smokier
- more antique
- more painterly
- more gothic
- less bright and less clean than earlier mascot palettes

The one exception is the eyes. They should stay vivid and readable, using the locked bright gold rather than the more muted sampled golds in the image.

# 3. Color Role Rules

## Skin

Lilith's skin should be dark oxblood red, not bright candy red, pink, orange, or purple.

Use:

- `#822220` as the main skin base
- `#8A2522` for stronger mid red areas
- `#621E19` and `#380F0D` for shadows
- `#1D0806` for the deepest red-black shadow cuts
- `#9A422D` only as a restrained warm highlight

The skin should remain readable, but it should feel smoky and painterly rather than clean, glossy, or saturated.

## Hair and Clothing

Lilith's hair, dress, gloves, boots, and other black materials should be blackened charcoal rather than pure flat black everywhere.

Use:

- `#0A0807` as the main black
- `#050403` for the deepest visible black areas
- `#0F0C0B` and `#14110F` for form separation
- `#181412` and `#2B1812` for warm painterly edges

Hair and clothing should stay dark enough to feel gothic, but not so flat that the silhouette or shape disappears.

## Gold Jewelry

Lilith's jewelry should be antique copper-gold, not clean yellow.

Use:

- `#A56C40` as the gold base
- `#643F25` and `#211811` for gold shadows
- `#DBA068` for readable highlights
- `#F1BD7A` and `#F9D390` only for tiny bright glints

Gold should feel old, warm, and gothic. It should never overpower the eyes.

## Eyes

The eyes remain the exception to the sampled reference palette.

Use:

- `#FFC400` for Lilith's bright iris identity color

Do not mute the eyes down to match the jewelry or sampled golds. Lilith's gaze must remain sharp and readable at emote, icon, and reference-sheet size.

## Lips, Nails, Horns, and Fangs

- Lips remain black and clearly visible.
- Nails remain black.
- Horns remain charcoal-black with warm brown-black ridged highlights.
- Fangs remain warm ivory and visible.

Use `#FCE4AC` for fang readability, with `#F4DCA4` for softer fang shading.

# 4. Palette Application Rules

When applying this palette to Lilith:

1. Preserve the dark gothic comic-painterly style.
2. Use the uploaded turnaround reference as the palette authority.
3. Keep the overall image smoky, dark, and antique.
4. Keep skin in the dark oxblood family.
5. Keep hair and clothing in blackened charcoal.
6. Keep jewelry antique copper-gold.
7. Keep eyes brighter than every other gold using `#FFC400`.
8. Keep lips and nails black.
9. Keep fangs warm ivory and readable.
10. Use rusty crimson edge strokes for silhouette definition.
11. Avoid bright, clean, pastel, glossy, or generic chibi colors.

---

# 5. Palette Drift Warnings

Reject or revise an image if Lilith's palette starts to look:

- too bright red
- too pink
- too orange
- too purple
- too clean or glossy
- too pastel
- too gray and desaturated
- too flat black with no readable form
- too yellow-gold in the jewelry
- too muted in the eyes
- missing black lips
- missing black nails
- missing warm ivory fangs
- missing rusty crimson edge accents
