# Lilith Dark Painterly-Inky Style Source

## Document Status

**Version:** 1.1  
**Canon Stage:** Draft Canon Style Source  
**Source Image:** Lilith Expression Reference  
**Purpose:** Define the proper art style for Lilith expression references, emotes, icons, and mascot assets so future generations preserve the intended look without becoming too busy, too clean, too anime-generic, or too fully gritty.

This document focuses on **style and rendering treatment**, not redesigning Lilith. Character identity, palette, body, outfit, and expression rules should still follow the existing canon identity and palette source documents.

---

# 1. Style Name

**Dark Painterly-Inky Gothic Mascot Style**

This style is a controlled blend of:

- gothic comic art
- painterly mascot rendering
- simplified inky shadow shapes
- muted red-black-gold fantasy color design
- compact expression-reference readability

It should feel dark, sharp, and handmade, but still clean enough for reference sheets, mod icons, Discord emotes, and small mascot assets.

---

# 2. Overall Style Summary

Lilith should be rendered with **dark, simplified painterly-inky shading**.

The image should use bold black masses, smoky red shadows, restrained antique highlights, and thick silhouette-defining linework. Texture should exist, but it should support the shapes rather than cover them.

The correct read is:

**dark gothic, painterly, inky, sharp, readable, mascot-like, smug, and controlled.**

The incorrect read is:

**busy, scratchy, over-grunged, hyper-detailed, glossy anime, flat vector, soft pastel, or fully Darkest Dungeon.**

---

# 3. Core Rendering Traits

## Linework

Use thick, dark, rough-edged linework.

Required line qualities:

- thick black or near-black outer silhouette lines
- sharp red-brown edge accents in selected areas
- uneven hand-inked contour edges
- strong facial line clarity around eyes, lips, nose, and fangs
- clean enough to read at small sizes

Avoid:

- thin delicate anime outlines
- perfectly smooth vector outlines
- excessive sketch lines
- scratchy clutter on every edge
- outlines so black and heavy that the face disappears

## Shading

Use simplified painterly shading, not heavy texture everywhere.

Required shading qualities:

- large, readable shadow shapes
- smoky red-black shadow masses
- limited midtones
- restrained warm highlights
- dark cheek, neck, hair, and clothing shadows
- high contrast around the eyes, lips, and fangs

The shading should be **simpler than full painterly rendering** but more textured than flat cel shading.

Avoid:

- too many tiny speckles
- noisy grain across the whole image
- over-rendered skin texture
- glossy airbrush shine
- muddy low-contrast shading
- flat vector blocks with no painterly character

## Texture

Texture should be subtle and purposeful.

Use texture mainly for:

- hair mass separation
- horn ridges
- clothing folds
- smoky shadow transitions
- small edge imperfections
- background atmosphere

Texture should not dominate the image. The correct texture level is **light to moderate**, with clear forms still visible.

Avoid:

- dense scratch marks
- heavy grunge overlays
- dirt-like skin grain
- random speckles on every surface
- background noise that competes with Lilith

---

# 4. Shape Language

Lilith’s shapes should be compact, sharp, and readable.

Required shape traits:

- large dark hair masses with a strong side-swept silhouette
- simplified hair clumps instead of individual strands everywhere
- sharp heavy-lidded eye shapes
- large readable black lips
- small visible fangs
- curved black horns with simple ridged bands
- pointed ears with clean red silhouettes
- gothic neckline and jewelry kept readable

The style should preserve Lilith’s mascot clarity. Detail should never make the face harder to recognize.

---

# 5. Face Rendering Rules

The face is the highest-priority area.

## Eyes

Lilith’s eyes should stay:

- narrow
- sharp
- heavy-lidded
- bright gold
- slightly sleepy
- smug and focused

Use strong dark upper eyelids and small bright gold irises. The eye highlight should be minimal but readable.

Avoid large round eyes, innocent eyes, soft sparkly anime eyes, or eyes that become muted and unreadable.

## Lips

Lilith’s black lips are a defining feature.

Required lip treatment:

- larger than typical chibi lips
- black or near-black
- sharp and readable
- clear upper/lower lip shape
- visible even in subtle expressions
- integrated with fangs and smirks

Avoid tiny dot mouths, invisible lips, soft pink lips, or generic anime mouth shapes.

## Fangs and Tongue

Fangs should be small but clear, warm ivory, and visible when the mouth opens.

For tongue expressions:

- use a short demon tongue
- make the fork clear but simple
- keep the tongue close to the mouth
- avoid long snake-like curls
- avoid messy red smears

---

# 6. Hair Rendering Rules

Lilith’s hair should read as a large black painterly mass.

Required hair treatment:

- blackened charcoal base
- big side-swept bang covering part of the face
- chunky painterly hair groups
- minimal strand clutter
- warm red-brown edge accents
- subtle charcoal highlights

Hair should feel gothic and smoky, not glossy or silky.

Avoid:

- bright shine streaks
- hundreds of fine strands
- gray washed-out hair
- flat pure-black hair with no shape
- symmetrical generic anime hair

---

# 7. Skin Rendering Rules

Lilith’s skin should be dark oxblood red with controlled painterly form.

Required skin treatment:

- deep red base
- smoky red-black shadows
- minimal texture
- restrained warm highlights
- clear face planes without over-rendering

Skin should be smoother than the hair and outfit. Speckles or rough texture should be very limited.

Avoid:

- bright candy red
- pink or orange skin
- heavy dirt texture
- glossy airbrushed skin
- too much mottling or grain

---

# 8. Clothing, Horns, and Jewelry Rendering

## Clothing

Clothing should be near-black gothic fabric with simplified painterly folds.

Use:

- large dark masses
- red-brown edge strokes
- minimal fold detail
- readable ruffle or lace shapes

Avoid over-detailing the ruffles or making the outfit busier than the face.

## Horns

Horns should be dark charcoal-black with readable ridges.

Use:

- dark curved silhouettes
- simple segmented ridge bands
- muted warm edge highlights

Avoid bright horns, smooth plastic horns, or tiny unreadable ridges.

## Jewelry

Jewelry should be antique copper-gold and secondary to the eyes.

Use:

- small readable diamond shapes
- muted gold highlights
- tiny bright glints only when needed

Avoid clean yellow-gold, oversized jewelry, or jewelry that steals focus from the face.

---

# 9. Texture Budget

This style needs a clear texture budget.

## Correct Texture Amount

Use approximately:

- **70% large clean shadow shapes**
- **20% painterly edge strokes**
- **10% small texture/noise details**

Texture should be concentrated near silhouette edges, hair breaks, horns, and deep shadows.

## Too Busy

The image is too busy if:

- skin has lots of speckles
- hair is covered in tiny scratches
- every edge has red rim strokes
- the background competes with the character
- the face becomes harder to read
- expressions lose clarity

## Too Clean

The image is too clean if:

- it looks like smooth vector art
- the shadows are flat and sterile
- the hair has no painterly mass
- the gothic mood disappears
- it looks like generic clean anime

---

# 10. Background and Layout Style

For expression sheets, the background should be simple and dark.

Recommended:

- black or near-black background
- very subtle smoky texture only
- minimal or no decorative framing
- clean 2x2 or 4x3 layout
- small readable labels
- labels in muted antique gold or warm ivory

Avoid:

- ornate poster frames
- sparkles and magical effects
- large decorative borders
- noisy grunge background
- bright red backgrounds that compete with the skin

# 11. Prompt Block: Style Only

Use this block when the character identity is already established and only the style needs to be preserved:

```text
Render in Lilith’s dark painterly-inky gothic mascot style: thick rough dark outlines, simplified smoky red-black shadows, large readable black hair masses, restrained painterly texture, muted antique copper-gold highlights, dark oxblood skin, black lips, warm ivory fangs, and bright gold heavy-lidded eyes.

Use clean readable shading with controlled texture. Keep the image dark, gothic, inky, and painterly, but do not make it overly busy. Use large shadow shapes first, subtle texture second, and tiny scratches or speckles only sparingly.

Do not make it flat vector art, glossy anime, soft pastel, hyper-realistic, heavily grunged, noisy, muddy, or fully Darkest Dungeon. Preserve mascot readability and expression clarity.
```

---

# 12. Prompt Block: Expression Sheet Style

Use this block for expression references:

```text
Create a clean Lilith expression reference sheet in her dark painterly-inky gothic mascot style.

Use simple head-and-shoulders panels on a dark background. Keep the rendering readable and controlled: thick dark outlines, large smoky red-black shadow shapes, blackened charcoal hair, subtle painterly edge texture, restrained rusty red accents, muted antique copper-gold jewelry, dark oxblood skin, black readable lips, warm ivory fangs, and sharp bright gold heavy-lidded eyes.

Keep texture light to moderate. Avoid excessive speckling, scratch marks, grunge overlays, busy skin grain, ornate frames, sparkles, props, or poster-style decoration. The sheet should feel like an official canon expression reference, not a decorative illustration poster.
```

---

# 13. Prompt Block: Texture Correction

Use this block when an image is close but too busy:

```text
Preserve the current image, composition, character design, face, expressions, lips, fangs, eyes, hair, horns, ears, outfit, jewelry, labels, and layout.

Reduce the visual noise and simplify the texture by about 25–35%.

Keep Lilith’s dark painterly-inky gothic mascot style: thick dark outlines, smoky red-black shadows, large black hair masses, muted antique gold accents, and warm red-brown edge strokes.

Remove excess speckling, tiny scratch marks, dense skin grain, and overly busy hair texture. Do not make it flat vector art or clean glossy anime. The final image should remain dark, painterly, and inky, but cleaner and more readable.
```

---

# 14. Prompt Block: Adding Back Inky Style Without Overdoing It

Use this block when an image has become too clean:

```text
Preserve the current image and all character details.

Add back Lilith’s dark painterly-inky gothic style in a controlled way. Strengthen the thick dark outlines, deepen the smoky red-black shadows, add subtle painterly texture to the hair and clothing, and add restrained warm red-brown edge strokes.

Keep the shading simple and readable. Do not add heavy grunge, dense speckling, scratchy noise, ornate effects, or excessive texture. The result should be darker, inkier, and more gothic while still clean enough for a character reference sheet.
```

---

# 15. Negative Prompt / Avoid List

Avoid these style drifts:

- overly busy texture
- dense speckling
- scratchy all-over grunge
- fully Darkest Dungeon rendering
- hyper-detailed fantasy painting
- glossy anime finish
- soft pastel anime
- flat vector art
- corporate mascot smoothness
- generic mobile-game chibi style
- bright candy colors
- washed-out gray blacks
- clean yellow jewelry
- muted dull eyes
- tiny invisible lips
- hidden fangs
- innocent or childlike expression language
- overly seductive expression language
- decorative poster design overpowering the reference sheet

---

# 16. Style Acceptance Checklist

Before approving a Lilith image, check:

- Are the outlines thick, dark, and slightly rough?
- Are the shadow shapes large and readable?
- Is the texture present but not noisy?
- Does the hair read as black painterly masses?
- Are the eyes bright gold, sharp, and heavy-lidded?
- Are the black lips large and readable?
- Are the fangs visible when appropriate?
- Is the palette dark oxblood, blackened charcoal, and antique gold?
- Is the image gothic and inky without becoming too gritty?
- Is the image clean enough to work as a reference sheet or emote?
- Does Lilith still feel smug, clever, and mischievous?

If the image fails because it is too busy, simplify texture before changing the character. If it fails because it is too clean, add controlled inky shadow and painterly edges without adding noise.

---

# 17. Final Style Summary

Lilith’s proper style is **dark painterly-inky gothic mascot art**.

It should use:

**thick dark outlines, large smoky red-black shadows, blackened charcoal hair and clothing, restrained painterly texture, rusty red-brown edge accents, antique copper-gold highlights, dark oxblood skin, black readable lips, warm ivory fangs, and bright gold heavy-lidded eyes.**

The most important balance is:

**inky but not noisy**  
**painterly but not over-rendered**  
**gothic but still mascot-readable**  
**dark but not muddy**  
**sharp and smug, never innocent or generic**
