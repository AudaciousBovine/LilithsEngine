## Purpose

This document provides reusable prompting templates for generating, revising, and reviewing Lilith artwork.

Use these templates alongside the canonical Lilith source files:

- Project Master Instruction
- Canon Character Guide
- Character Requirements
- Canon Color Palette
- Style Guide
- Body Proportion Guide
- Design Rules / Drift Prevention
- Future Expression Bible, Pose Bible, Variant Guide, and Cookbook Art Guide when available

These templates are not a replacement for the canon sources. They are practical prompt structures designed to help future artwork preserve Lilith's identity.

---

# How To Use These Templates

For most image prompts, use this structure:

1. Start with the task.
2. Add the Canon Lilith Lock.
3. Add the format or composition request.
4. Add the pose, expression, or activity.
5. Add the style lock.
6. Add the drift-prevention block.
7. Add any special constraints.

Recommended order:

```text
[Task]
[Canon Lilith Lock]
[Format / Composition]
[Pose / Expression / Activity]
[Style Lock]
[Color Lock]
[Proportion Lock]
[Drift Prevention / Avoid]
[Output Constraints]
```

The strongest prompts are usually clear, direct, and not overloaded with unnecessary decorations.

# Face Identity Lock

Use when the face keeps drifting.

```text
Preserve Lilith's canon face identity above all else: heavy upper eyelids, sharp gold eyes, black visible lips, small visible fangs, black sidebang silhouette, red skin, pointed ears, black horns, and a slightly smug mischievous expression. Do not soften her into a generic cute character. Do not remove the black lips or fangs.
```

---

# Proportion Lock

Use when Lilith becomes too tall, thin, or realistic.

```text
Use compact mature-chibi mascot proportions: oversized head, short compact body, short legs, small torso, sturdy readable silhouette, simplified limbs, no elongated torso, no long legs, no tall or thin redesign, no fashion-doll proportions, no realistic anatomy. Preserve the same compact body proportions as the canon character reference.
```

---

# No-Wings / No-Tail Proportion Lock

Use when removing wings and/or tail.

```text
Remove only the requested wings and/or tail. Do not redesign Lilith. Removing wings or tail must not change her body proportions, face, outfit, colors, expression, or personality. Do not make her taller, thinner, narrower, softer, or more realistic.
```

---

# Style Lock

Use this block for most artwork.

```text
Clean mascot-vector cartoon style, thick clean outlines, simple readable shapes, flat colors or simple cel shading, minimal detail, strong silhouette, sticker-like clarity, Discord-emote readability, easy to redraw, no painterly rendering, no realism, no soft airbrush shading, no complex textures.
```

---

# Color Lock

Use when colors drift.

```text
Use Lilith's canon red-black-gold palette: red skin, black hair, black horns, black clothing, black lips, black nails, gold eyes, gold jewelry, red tail when included, black-and-red bat wings when included. Do not shift her skin toward pink, purple, orange, brown, gray, or human tones. Do not change black lips to red or pink. Do not change gold jewelry to silver.
```

---

# Drift Prevention Block

Use at the end of most prompts.

```text
Avoid design drift: no missing black lips, no missing fangs, no round innocent eyes, no generic anime face, no tall or thin body, no long legs, no long torso, no realistic anatomy, no seductive succubus styling, no grimdark horror styling, no painterly rendering, no complex textures, no busy background, no props covering the face.
```

---

# Output Cleanliness Block

Use for reference sheets, emotes, icons, and source art.

```text
Keep the composition clean and uncluttered. Lilith must remain the focal point. The face, eyes, black lips, fangs, sidebang, horns, and compact chibi silhouette must be clearly readable. Use a simple or transparent background unless a background is explicitly requested.
```

# Final Prompting Rule

A good Lilith prompt should protect the character before describing the scene.

The safest order is:

```text
Lilith identity first.
Face identity second.
Body proportions third.
Style and palette fourth.
Pose, prop, or scene fifth.
Drift prevention last.
```

When a prompt becomes too long, do not remove the face identity, black lips, fangs, gold eyes, sidebang, compact proportions, or mischievous personality. Remove background and prop detail first.
