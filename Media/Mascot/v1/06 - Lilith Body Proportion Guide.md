# LILITH - BODY PROPORTION GUIDE

## Purpose

This document defines Lilith's canonical body proportions and proportion-control rules.

It exists to prevent Lilith from drifting into a taller, thinner, more realistic, more fashion-doll-like, or less chibi body type across future artwork.

The Canon Character Guide defines who Lilith is.  
The Character Requirements define what must be present.  
The Canon Color Palette defines exact colors.  
The Style Guide defines how Lilith should be rendered.  
The Design Rules / Drift Prevention document defines what mistakes to reject.  
This Body Proportion Guide defines how Lilith's body should be shaped, scaled, and kept consistent.

Lilith should always feel like a compact mature-chibi mascot character, not a realistic fantasy woman.

"Mature-chibi" means Lilith is not baby-like or toddler-like, but she still keeps mascot proportions: large head, compact body, short legs, simplified anatomy, and no realistic or fashion-doll body structure. Do not interpret mature-chibi as taller, thinner, bustier, more elegant, or more realistic.

# Core Proportion Identity

Lilith's body type is:

- Compact
- Mature-chibi
- Mascot-like
- Short-bodied
- Short-legged
- Wide and sturdy enough to read clearly
- Simplified and easy to redraw

Lilith is not:

- Tall
- Thin
- Long-legged
- Long-torsoed
- Fashion-doll proportioned
- Realistically proportioned
- Hypersexualized
- Fragile or waif-like
- Stretched to fit a pose

Her proportions should support her mischievous mascot identity.

---

# Primary Proportion Rule

Lilith's head and face must visually dominate the character.

The body exists to support the face, expression, and silhouette.

When viewing the full character, the first read should be:

1. Face
2. Hair and horns
3. Compact body silhouette
4. Outfit
5. Wings and tail, if included
6. Props or background

If the viewer notices long legs, a tall body, a narrow waist, or realistic anatomy before the face, the proportions have drifted.

---

# Visual Ratio Target

These are visual targets, not strict pixel measurements.

For canon Lilith artwork:

- The head and hair mass should be the largest visual shape.
- The body below the head should feel short and compact.
- The full figure should feel closer to a chibi mascot than a normal human.
- The legs should be visibly short compared to the head.
- The torso should be short and simplified.
- The silhouette should feel sturdy, not narrow or elongated.

A useful proportion target:

- Head and hair mass: approximately half of the character's visual height, excluding extreme horn height.
- Body below the chin: approximately half of the character's visual height.
- Legs: short and supportive, not the dominant feature.
- Torso and dress: compact and simplified.

Do not use exact mathematical measurement as an excuse to make her taller or thinner. The correct read is visual: compact mature-chibi mascot.

---

# Head Proportions

The head is the anchor of Lilith's design.

Required:

- Large head relative to the body
- Slightly narrower face shape compared to a generic round chibi
- Face large enough for heavy-lidded eyes, black lips, and fangs to remain readable
- Hair mass large enough to create the black sidebang silhouette
- Horns readable but not so large that they replace the head as the dominant shape

Avoid:

- Tiny head
- Realistic head-to-body ratio
- Overly round baby-like face
- Long narrow realistic face
- Head shape that removes the sidebang identity
- Horns so large that the character becomes top-heavy in the wrong way

Lilith's head should feel large, expressive, and iconic.

---

# Face Shape

Lilith's face should be chibi-stylized but not generic.

Required:

- Slightly narrowed face compared to a standard round chibi
- Strong eye area
- Clear mouth area for black lips and fangs
- Red face framed by black hair and sidebang
- Shape that supports a smug, mischievous expression

Avoid:

- Fully round baby face
- Tall realistic face
- Overly pointed realistic chin
- Soft innocent face shape
- Face shape that makes her look like a different demon girl

The face should remain expressive, compact, and mischievous.

---

# Neck and Shoulder Proportions

Lilith's neck and shoulders should stay simplified.

Required:

- Short or minimally visible neck
- Compact shoulder width
- Shoulders simplified by the outfit and hair
- Upper body should not look realistic or elongated

Avoid:

- Long graceful neck
- Broad realistic shoulders
- Narrow fashion-model shoulders that make the body look tall
- Detailed collarbone anatomy
- Realistic upper-body structure

The neck and shoulders should not pull the design toward realism.

---

# Torso Proportions

Lilith's torso must remain short and compact.

Required:

- Short torso
- Small compact upper body
- Simplified waist area
- Dress shape should support a sturdy chibi silhouette
- Torso should not become the visual focus

Avoid:

- Long torso
- Realistic ribcage or waist anatomy
- Narrow hourglass redesign
- Fashion-doll torso
- Hypersexualized chest or waist emphasis
- Torso stretched to fit a pose

The torso should feel like a simple support structure for the head and expression.

---

# Arm Proportions

Lilith's arms should be short, simplified, and pose-readable.

Required:

- Short chibi arms
- Simple limb shapes
- Hands large enough to read gestures
- Five fingers when hands are clearly visible
- Black nails when visible
- Arms should support the pose without stretching the body

Avoid:

- Long realistic arms
- Thin elegant arms
- Overly detailed arm anatomy
- Realistic elbows, wrists, or musculature
- Arms that make her proportions look taller
- Hands covering the face identity

Hands and arms should be expressive but secondary to the face.

---

# Hand Proportions

Lilith's hands should be simplified mascot hands with clear nails.

Required:

- Slightly oversized enough to read gestures
- Five fingers when clearly visible
- Black nails
- Simple shapes
- Readable silhouette

Avoid:

- Tiny realistic hands
- Four-finger cartoon hands
- Missing nails
- Long horror claws
- Overly complex finger anatomy
- Hands that obscure eyes, lips, fangs, or sidebang

Hands should communicate attitude, not realism.

---

# Hip, Dress, and Lower Body Shape

Lilith's lower body should remain compact and readable.

Required:

- Simple black dress silhouette
- Lower body mostly simplified by the outfit
- Sturdy stance
- Compact lower-body mass
- No excessive anatomical detail

Avoid:

- Realistic hip anatomy
- Long fashion silhouette
- Narrow body column
- Thin waist-to-leg transition
- Outfit shape that makes her look tall
- Excessive skirt or fabric detail that hides the chibi body read

The outfit should reinforce compactness.

---

# Leg Proportions

Lilith's legs must remain short.

Required:

- Short chibi legs
- Compact stance
- Legs support the body rather than dominate it
- Boots should feel simple and readable
- Leg length should not make her taller or more elegant

Avoid:

- Long legs
- Thin legs
- Realistic thigh/calf anatomy
- Fashion-model proportions
- Tall boot shapes that elongate the body too much
- Poses that stretch the legs for elegance

Leg drift is one of the fastest ways Lilith stops feeling like a mascot.

---

# Foot and Boot Proportions

Lilith's feet and boots should be simple and supportive.

Required:

- Small-to-medium chibi boot shapes
- Readable base for the pose
- Simple dark footwear
- Boots should not elongate the legs too much

Avoid:

- Very long feet
- Realistic high-fashion heels dominating the silhouette
- Tall thin boots that make the legs look long
- Overly detailed footwear
- Foot shapes that make her stance unstable

Boots should complete the silhouette, not change the body type.

---

# Silhouette Width

Lilith should not become too narrow.

Required:

- Wide enough silhouette to feel sturdy and mascot-like
- Hair, horns, outfit, wings, or tail may help widen the silhouette
- Body should feel compact rather than vertical
- Stance should feel grounded

Avoid:

- Thin vertical silhouette
- Narrow fashion-doll shape
- Extremely small shoulders and hips under a large head
- Removing wings or tail and then narrowing the body
- Poses that compress her into a thin line

Compact does not mean tiny and fragile. It means short, sturdy, readable, and mascot-like.

---

# Front View Rules

In front view, preserve:

- Large head
- Strong hair silhouette
- Clear horns
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Symmetrical or near-symmetrical stance
- Readable black outfit shape
- Black lips and fangs visible
- Gold eyes clearly visible

Avoid:

- Narrowing the body too much
- Making the legs longer for elegance
- Hiding the hands in ways that remove black nails from every front view
- Making the head smaller to show more outfit

Front view should be the clearest proportion reference.

---

# Three-Quarter View Rules

In three-quarter view, preserve:

- Same body height as front view
- Same compact head-to-body relationship
- Same short legs
- Same sturdy silhouette
- Same sidebang identity
- Face clearly visible

Avoid:

- Making the three-quarter view taller than the front view
- Slimming the torso to imply depth
- Lengthening the legs for perspective
- Losing the sidebang shape
- Letting the far side of the body disappear too much

Three-quarter view should add dimension, not redesign the proportions.

---

# Side View Rules

In side view, preserve:

- Large head mass
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Clear nose/mouth area with black lips where visible
- Horn and hair silhouette
- Sturdy stance

Avoid:

- Turning the side view into a realistic profile
- Long neck
- Long torso
- Long legs
- Excessive chest or hip emphasis
- Thin vertical silhouette

Side view should still read as the same compact mascot.

---

# Back View Rules

In back view, preserve:

- Large head and hair mass
- Black hair silhouette
- Black horns
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Simple outfit silhouette
- Wings and tail when included

Avoid:

- Making the back view taller or thinner
- Changing hair length or silhouette drastically
- Adding realistic back anatomy
- Making the dress or legs longer than in front view

Back view must match the same body proportions as the other views.

---

# Wing Proportion Rules

Wings are secondary to Lilith's body and face.

When wings are included:

- Wings should support the silhouette.
- Wings should be readable but not oversized.
- Wings should not make the character look taller.
- Wings should not obscure the face.
- Wings should not force the body to become thinner.

Avoid:

- Giant wings that dominate the character
- Wings that hide the compact body shape
- Wings that create a tall vertical silhouette
- Overly complex wing anatomy

Wings are part of canon Lilith, but they do not define her proportions.

---

# Tail Proportion Rules

The tail is a secondary silhouette feature.

When the tail is included:

- Tail should be black with a spaded tip.
- Tail should be simple and readable.
- Tail should support the pose and silhouette.
- Tail should not overpower the body.
- Tail should not compensate for weak body proportions.

Avoid:

- Huge tail dominating the image
- Long tail that distracts from the compact body
- Tail used to visually stretch the character
- Tail shape that becomes more important than face or body

The tail should add attitude, not redesign the silhouette.

---

# No-Wings / No-Tail Proportion Rules

Removing wings or tail must not change Lilith's body type.

If wings or tail are removed:

- Keep the same head size.
- Keep the same torso length.
- Keep the same leg length.
- Keep the same body width.
- Keep the same chibi compactness.
- Keep the same outfit shape.
- Keep the same face identity.
- Do not add height to compensate.
- Do not make the body narrower to compensate.

A no-wing or no-tail Lilith is still canon Lilith with parts removed, not a redesigned Lilith.

---

# Pose-Based Proportion Rules

Dynamic poses may bend, twist, lean, or exaggerate movement, but they must not change the character's base proportions.

Allowed:

- Leaning
- Strutting
- Running
- Sneaking
- Presenting
- Laughing
- Pointing
- Arms crossed
- Hand on hip
- Looking over shoulder
- Cooking or holding props

Required:

- Head remains large.
- Body remains compact.
- Legs remain short.
- Torso remains short.
- Silhouette remains mascot-like.
- Face remains readable whenever possible.

Avoid:

- Stretching the legs for action
- Stretching the torso for elegance
- Shrinking the head for complex poses
- Making the body thin to fit a gesture
- Using realistic anatomy to make the pose work

The pose should adapt to Lilith's body. Lilith's body should not adapt into another character to serve the pose.

---

# Expression Sheet Proportion Rules

For expression sheets:

- Keep the same head shape across expressions.
- Keep the same face proportions across expressions.
- Keep the sidebang silhouette consistent.
- Keep horns consistent in size and placement.
- Do not stretch the face for exaggerated emotion.
- Do not enlarge the eyes into generic anime eyes.
- Do not shrink the mouth so black lips and fangs disappear.

Expression variation should happen inside Lilith's established face structure.

---

# Pose Sheet Proportion Rules

For pose sheets:

- Every pose should use the same base body height.
- Every pose should use the same head-to-body relationship.
- Legs should remain equally short across poses.
- Torso length should remain consistent.
- Hands, boots, horns, and hair should remain consistent.
- Scale should not change between poses unless intentionally labeled.

Reject pose sheets where one pose looks like a taller, thinner, older, or more realistic version of Lilith.

---

# Reference Sheet Proportion Rules

For body reference sheets:

- Front, three-quarter, side, and back views must share the same height.
- Head size must remain consistent across views.
- Torso length must remain consistent across views.
- Leg length must remain consistent across views.
- Hair mass must remain consistent across views.
- Outfit length must remain consistent across views.
- Wings and tail, if included, must not distort the base body proportions.

A reference sheet should lock the body design, not explore variations.

---

# Emote and Icon Proportion Rules

For emotes and icons:

- Face should dominate the composition.
- Body may be reduced or cropped.
- Eyes, black lips, and fangs must remain readable.
- Body proportions should still feel compact if visible.
- Hands may be enlarged slightly for gesture readability.

Avoid:

- Showing too much full body if it makes the face unreadable
- Shrinking the head to fit wings, tail, or props
- Letting props dominate the face
- Losing the chibi body read when body is visible

For emotes, face readability is more important than showing the full body.

---

# Cookbook and Scene Art Proportion Rules

For cookbook, cooking, or scene illustrations:

- Waist-up Lilith is often preferred.
- Head and face should remain dominant.
- Upper body should stay compact.
- Arms should be simplified.
- Props should not force realistic anatomy.
- Table, cauldron, cookbook, or ingredients should not obscure face identity.

Avoid:

- Realistic seated or standing anatomy
- Long arms reaching unnaturally around props
- Tall torso behind a table
- Props hiding the mouth, fangs, lips, or eyes
- Scene composition that makes Lilith feel less chibi

Scene art should preserve the same mascot proportions as reference art.

---

# Common Proportion Drift Problems

Reject or revise artwork if Lilith becomes:

- Too tall
- Too thin
- Too elegant
- Too realistic
- Too long-legged
- Too long-torsoed
- Too fashion-doll-like
- Too anatomically mature
- Too narrow after removing wings or tail
- Too stretched by a dynamic pose
- Too small-headed
- Too baby-faced and round
- Too fragile or waif-like
- Too complex in anatomy
- Too focused on body shape instead of face identity

These are not minor style choices. They change the character read.

---

# Proportion Correction Order

When revising proportion drift, fix issues in this order:

1. Restore the large head and face dominance.
2. Restore compact body height.
3. Shorten legs if they became too long.
4. Shorten torso if it became too long.
5. Widen or stabilize the silhouette if it became too narrow.
6. Simplify anatomy if it became realistic.
7. Restore the outfit shape if it became too fashion-like.
8. Recheck face identity, lips, fangs, eyes, and sidebang.
9. Recheck wings and tail only after the base body is correct.
10. Recheck emote readability at small size.

Do not fix proportion drift by adding more details. Fix the base shapes first.

---

# Fast Proportion Prompt Language

Use this language when generating or revising Lilith:

> Compact mature-chibi mascot proportions, oversized head, short compact body, short legs, small torso, sturdy readable silhouette, simplified limbs, no elongated torso, no long legs, no tall or thin redesign, no fashion-doll proportions, preserve the same body proportions as the canon reference.

For no-wing or no-tail variants, add:

> Removing wings or tail must not change her body proportions. Do not make her taller, thinner, narrower, or more realistic.

For pose sheets, add:

> Keep consistent head size, body height, torso length, leg length, and compact chibi proportions across every pose.

---

# Final Proportion Approval Checklist

Before approving Lilith artwork, check:

1. Does the head visually dominate the character?
2. Does the body feel compact and mascot-like?
3. Are the legs short?
4. Is the torso short?
5. Does the silhouette feel sturdy rather than narrow?
6. Does she avoid realistic anatomy?
7. Does she avoid fashion-doll proportions?
8. Does she avoid tall or thin redesign drift?
9. Are the front, side, three-quarter, and back views consistent when shown together?
10. Do no-wing or no-tail variants keep the same body proportions?
11. Do poses preserve her base body type?
12. Does the artwork still prioritize the face over the body?
13. Would the body still read clearly at small size?
14. Does the final character still feel like canon Lilith?

If any answer is no, revise the proportions before accepting the artwork.

---

# Final Rule

Lilith's body should always serve her identity as a mischievous chibi demoness mascot.

The correct proportion read is:

> Large head, compact body, short legs, sturdy silhouette, mischievous face first.

If the body becomes tall, thin, elegant, realistic, or fashion-like, it is no longer supporting canon Lilith.
