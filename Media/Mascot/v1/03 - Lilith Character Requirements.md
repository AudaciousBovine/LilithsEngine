# LILITH - CHARACTER REQUIREMENTS

## Purpose

This document defines the required visual and personality traits that must be present for artwork to read as canon Lilith.

The Canon Character Guide defines who Lilith is.
The Character Requirements define what must be present.
The Canon Color Palette defines exact colors.
The Style Guide defines how Lilith should be rendered.
The Body Proportion Guide defines detailed body proportion rules.
The Design Rules / Drift Prevention document defines what mistakes to reject.

Use this document as an objective checklist when creating, reviewing, revising, or approving Lilith artwork.

This document overrides broad stylistic experimentation, but it does not override the Face Reference, Character Reference, or exact Canon Color Palette.

---

# Requirement Levels

Lilith's requirements are divided into four levels.

## Level 1 - Identity-Critical Requirements

If these are missing or changed, the artwork no longer reads as canon Lilith.

## Level 2 - Recognition Requirements

These features make Lilith recognizable from silhouette, thumbnail, icon size, or quick visual read.

## Level 3 - Design Consistency Requirements

These features keep Lilith's outfit, accessories, anatomy, and secondary design details consistent.

## Level 4 - Style Requirements

These requirements control how Lilith is drawn so she remains reproducible, emote-friendly, and visually consistent.

---

# Level 1 - Identity-Critical Requirements

These are non-negotiable.

Every canon Lilith artwork must preserve the following:

- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Black hair
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp mischievous eyes
- Slight smug or amused expression
- Pointed ears
- Black horns
- Mischievous personality read

If any of these are missing, unclear, or replaced, the artwork must be revised.

---

# Face Requirements

Lilith's face carries most of her identity.

Required face traits:

- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp eye shape
- Confident gaze
- Slightly raised or expressive brows
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Black sidebang framing the face
- Pointed ears
- Black horns
- Slight smugness or mischievous amusement

The face must remain recognizable even when the pose, expression, outfit, or activity changes.

Reject if:

- Eyes become large, round, innocent, or generic
- Eyelids are missing or too open by default
- Lips are missing, too small, pink, red, or not visibly black
- Fangs are missing in expressions where they should be visible
- Sidebang silhouette disappears
- Expression becomes sad, helpless, blank, or generic cute
- Face reads as a different demon girl instead of Lilith

---

# Eye Requirements

Lilith's eyes are one of her strongest identity markers.

Required:

- Gold irises
- Sharp shape
- Heavy upper eyelids
- Confident eye contact or deliberate sideways gaze
- Slight mischievous or knowing expression
- Eyebrows readable enough to support the expression

Avoid:

- Large innocent anime eyes
- Round childlike eyes
- Blank doll eyes
- Soft helpless gaze
- Pure rage without mischief
- Eye colors other than canon gold

The eyes should make Lilith feel clever, amused, and self-assured.

---

# Mouth, Lip, and Fang Requirements

Black lips are mandatory.

Required:

- Lips must be black
- Lips must be clearly visible
- Lips must remain readable at small sizes
- Lip shape must survive open-mouth expressions
- Small fangs should be visible in most expressions
- Fangs should be subtle, cute, and mischievous

Avoid:

- Missing lips
- Red, pink, purple, or natural-colored lips
- Mouth shapes that erase the black lip silhouette
- Missing fangs
- Huge vampire fangs
- Monster teeth
- Overly sharp horror teeth

If the expression makes the mouth open, the black lip shape should still be readable.

---

# Personality Requirements

Lilith must feel like the same character regardless of pose or activity.

Required personality read:

- Sassy
- Mischievous
- Clever
- Confident
- Self-assured
- Playful villain
- Trickster
- Slightly amused
- Pleased with herself

Lilith should appear as though she knows something the viewer does not.

Avoid personality drift into:

- Generic demoness
- Seductive succubus
- Grimdark villain
- Cruel monster
- Sad character
- Depressed character
- Helpless innocent character
- Random angry character
- Realistic fantasy woman

Mischievous amusement is required even in action, reaction, cooking, reading, presenting, or movement poses.

---

# Level 2 - Recognition Requirements

These traits make Lilith recognizable immediately.

Required recognition features:

- Large mature-chibi head
- Compact mascot body
- Short legs
- Wide readable silhouette
- Black horns
- Black sidebang
- Red skin
- Black hair
- Black outfit
- Gold eyes
- Black lips
- Gold jewelry or gold accents
- Bat wings when included
- Black spaded tail when included

At 64x64 or 128x128 size, Lilith should still be identifiable by her face, colors, horns, sidebang, and silhouette.

---

# Body Proportion Requirements

Lilith uses compact mature-chibi mascot proportions.

Required:

- Large head relative to body
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Small torso
- Simplified limbs
- Wide or sturdy silhouette
- Slightly narrower face without becoming realistic
- Body should feel mascot-like, not fashion-like

Avoid:

- Tall redesigns
- Thin redesigns
- Long legs
- Long torso
- Realistic proportions
- Fashion-doll proportions
- Tiny head with realistic body ratio
- Hypersexualized anatomy
- Overly mature fantasy anatomy

Removing wings or tail must not make the body taller, thinner, narrower, or more realistic.

---

# Silhouette Requirements

Lilith must be recognizable from silhouette alone.

Required silhouette traits:

- Large head
- Small compact body
- Black horn shapes
- Strong black sidebang shape
- Gothic black outfit mass
- Bat wings when included
- Red spaded tail when included

Avoid:

- Weak or unclear horn shapes
- Missing sidebang silhouette
- Thin fashion-doll outline
- Overly complex wing silhouette
- Props covering the head or face
- Hair shapes that change her identity

If the silhouette no longer reads as Lilith, simplify the design.

---

# Level 3 - Design Consistency Requirements

These features may have small pose-based variation, but should remain consistent across canon artwork.

---

# Hair Requirements

Required:

- Black hair
- Clear sidebang silhouette
- Large simple hair masses
- Minimal strand detail
- Strong readable shape

Avoid:

- Highly detailed hair
- Realistic hair texture
- Excessive individual strands
- Flyaway hair clutter
- Hairstyles that remove the sidebang identity
- Hair colors other than black

Hair should read as a strong graphic shape first.

---

# Horn Requirements

Required:

- Black or near-black horns
- Simple readable horn shapes
- Consistent placement on the head
- Clear silhouette

Avoid:

- Missing horns
- Pale horns
- Overly complex horns
- Giant horns that dominate the design
- Horns that make her look like a different character

---

# Ear Requirements

Required:

- Pointed ears
- Readable elf/demon-like shape
- Consistent with chibi style

Avoid:

- Round human ears
- Ears hidden in every view
- Large monster ears that overpower the face

---

# Outfit Requirements

Canonical outfit identity:

- Black gothic dress or black gothic outfit
- Gold accents
- Gold jewelry
- Black stockings or dark legwear when visible
- Black boots or dark footwear when visible

Avoid:

- Replacing the outfit with unrelated clothing unless requested
- Overly complex fantasy armor
- Modern casual redesigns unless specifically requested as a variant
- Excessive lace, buckles, belts, or tiny details
- Outfit details that overpower the face

The outfit should support Lilith's identity, not become the main identity.

---

# Jewelry and Accessory Requirements

Required:

- Gold jewelry or gold accents
- Gold earrings when ears are visible and the composition allows
- Gold necklace or central gold accent when upper body is visible
- Gold decorative details should remain simple and readable

Avoid:

- Replacing gold with silver
- Overly complex jewelry
- Random unrelated accessories becoming canon
- Accessories that cover the face, lips, fangs, eyes, or sidebang

---

# Wing Requirements

Canon Lilith includes bat wings unless a no-wing variant is specifically requested.

Required when included:

- Bat-like wings
- Black outer structure
- Red inner membranes
- Simple readable shapes
- Wings should support the silhouette without overpowering the character

Avoid:

- Feathered wings
- Angel wings
- Overly complex dragon wings
- Giant wings that dominate the composition
- Wings that obscure the face
- Wrong wing colors

---

# Tail Requirements

Canon Lilith includes a black spaded tail unless a no-tail variant is specifically requested.

Required when included:

- Black tail
- Spaded tip
- Simple curved shape
- Readable silhouette

Avoid:

- Missing tail unless requested
- Forked tail
- Furry tail
- Overly complex tail
- Tail colors other than canon red
- Tail that distracts from the face

---

# Hand and Nail Requirements

Required:

- Five fingers when hands are clearly visible
- Black nails
- Simplified mascot-style hands
- Hands should support the expression or pose

Avoid:

- Four-finger cartoon hands
- Missing nails
- Realistic detailed hands
- Overly long horror claws
- Hands covering the face identity

---

# Level 4 - Style Requirements

Style requirements should follow the Style Guide, but the following are mandatory for character consistency.

Required:

- Mascot-style design
- Vector-cartoon influence
- Thick clean outlines
- Simple readable shapes
- Minimal visual clutter
- Flat color or simple cel shading
- Emote-friendly readability
- Easy-to-redraw design

Avoid:

- Realism
- Painterly rendering
- Soft airbrush rendering
- Complex textures
- Excessive detail
- Realistic lighting
- Semi-realistic fantasy illustration
- Over-rendered anime style

Lilith should remain simple enough to reproduce consistently.

---

# Expression Requirements

Expressions may vary, but the face must remain canon.

Required across most expressions:

- Gold eyes
- Heavy upper eyelids, unless widened eyes are necessary for the emotion
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs where possible
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Strong eyebrow readability
- Mischievous or knowing undertone

Expression-specific emotion is allowed, but it must not erase Lilith's identity.

Reject expressions where:

- Black lips vanish
- Fangs vanish without reason
- Eyes become generic
- The sidebang disappears
- Lilith becomes too innocent, sad, blank, or angry
- The expression reads as a different character

---

# Pose Requirements

Pose variety is allowed.

Required:

- Face remains visible whenever possible
- Expression remains readable
- Body remains compact and chibi
- Silhouette remains recognizable
- Hands and nails are correct when visible
- Props do not obscure identity

Avoid:

- Poses that stretch her body
- Poses that make her taller or thinner
- Poses that hide her face unnecessarily
- Poses that cover lips, fangs, eyes, or sidebang
- Poses that require realistic anatomy

The pose should serve Lilith. Lilith should not be redesigned to serve the pose.

---

# Prop Requirements

Props are secondary to Lilith.

Required:

- Props must not cover the face
- Props must not hide black lips, fangs, eyes, or sidebang
- Props should be simple and readable
- Props should support the activity or theme

Avoid:

- Oversized props dominating the composition
- Too many props
- Tiny decorative clutter
- Props that alter Lilith's silhouette too much
- Props that become more important than the character

When in doubt, simplify the prop before simplifying Lilith.

---

# Variant Requirements

Variants are allowed only when they preserve Lilith's identity.

A variant may change:

- Pose
- Expression
- Activity
- Prop
- Background
- Format
- Wing presence, if requested
- Tail presence, if requested
- Outfit theme, if explicitly requested

A variant must not change:

- Face identity
- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Black hair
- Black sidebang
- Black lips
- Small fangs
- Heavy-lidded mischievous gaze
- Compact chibi proportions
- Core red-black-gold color identity
- Mischievous personality read

No-wing or no-tail variants are still Lilith. They are not redesigns.

---

# Format-Specific Requirements

## Emotes and Icons

Required:

- Face dominant
- Expression readable
- Black lips visible
- Fangs visible where possible
- Strong silhouette
- Minimal detail
- Readable at 128x128

## Pose Sheets

Required:

- Consistent body proportions across poses
- Consistent scale
- Neutral or simple background
- Face visible in each pose where possible
- No pose should redesign the body

## Expression Sheets

Required:

- Consistent head shape
- Consistent facial features
- Black lips visible in every expression
- Fangs visible in most expressions
- Heavy-lidded identity preserved where possible
- Labels should not distract from the face

## Reference Sheets

Required:

- Clear front, side, three-quarter, or back views as needed
- Consistent proportions between views
- Canon colors
- Clean silhouette
- No unnecessary props or background clutter

## Cookbook or Scene Art

Required:

- Lilith remains focal point
- Face clearly visible
- Expression mischievous or amused
- Props simplified
- Background not too busy
- Canon colors and proportions preserved

# Required Lilith Test

When reviewing artwork, ask:

Does the viewer feel that Lilith knows something they do not?

The answer should be yes.

The correct read is not simply:

- Cute
- Sexy
- Evil
- Angry
- Sad
- Generic demon

The correct read is:

- Mischievous
- Confident
- Clever
- Pleased with herself
- Playfully villainous

---

# Top Five Identity Features

If every other detail had to be simplified, preserve these five features first:

1. Heavy-lidded gold eyes
2. Black visible lips
3. Small visible fangs
4. Smug mischievous expression
5. Black sidebang silhouette

These features are more important to Lilith's identity than wings, tail, props, background, or outfit detail.

---

# Automatic Revision Triggers

Revise the artwork if any of the following occur:

- Black lips are missing
- Fangs are missing in a face-focused image
- Gold eyes are missing or changed
- Heavy eyelids are missing from the default expression
- Sidebang silhouette is missing
- Body is too tall
- Body is too thin
- Legs are too long
- Torso is too long
- Skin color shifts away from canon red
- Hair is not black
- Jewelry is not gold
- Nails are missing or not black
- Wings are feathered
- Tail lacks spaded tip when included
- Style becomes painterly or realistic
- Detail level harms emote readability
- Lilith reads as seductive, sad, innocent, or generic instead of mischievous

These are not minor mistakes. They weaken character identity.

---

# Final Approval Checklist

A canon Lilith artwork must pass all core checks:

1. Does she immediately read as Lilith?
2. Are the heavy-lidded gold eyes present?
3. Are the black lips clearly visible?
4. Are small fangs visible where appropriate?
5. Is the black sidebang silhouette preserved?
6. Does she look mischievous, smug, amused, or self-assured?
7. Is the body compact mature-chibi rather than tall or thin?
8. Are the canon red, black, and gold colors preserved?
9. Are black nails present when hands are visible?
10. Are horns present and readable?
11. Are wings and tail correct when included?
12. Is the style simple, clean, and mascot-like?
13. Would the artwork still read at 128x128?
14. Could another artist redraw her easily?
15. Does the artwork avoid generic demoness or succubus drift?

If any core answer is no, revise the artwork before accepting it as canon Lilith.
