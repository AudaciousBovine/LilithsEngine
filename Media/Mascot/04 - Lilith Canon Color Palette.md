# LILITH - CANON COLOR PALETTE

## Purpose

This document defines Lilith's canonical color palette and color usage rules.

It exists to prevent color drift across future artwork, emotes, pose sheets, expression sheets, cookbook illustrations, icons, promotional art, reference sheets, and mod-related artwork.

The Canon Color Palette defines exact colors. It does not define Lilith's personality, proportions, or rendering style. Those are handled by the Canon Character Guide, Character Requirements, Style Guide, Body Proportion Guide, Design Rules / Drift Prevention, Expression Bible, and Pose Bible.

When in doubt, preserve Lilith's red skin, black hair, black lips, gold eyes, gold jewelry, and black clothing.

---

# Core Color Identity

Lilith's color identity is:

- Red skin
- Black hair
- Black horns
- Black clothing
- Black lips
- Black nails
- Gold eyes
- Gold jewelry
- Red tail
- Black and red bat wings

The dominant visual read should be red, black, and gold.

Avoid introducing major new colors into Lilith's body, face, outfit, or accessories unless the user explicitly requests a non-canon variant.

# Identity-Critical Colors

These colors are non-negotiable for canon Lilith.

## Skin

- Canon color: #CC2828
- Skin shadow: #B71C1C
- Lilith's skin should read as saturated red.
- Do not shift her skin toward pink, purple, orange, brown, gray, or pale human tones.

## Eyes

- Canon color: #FFC400
- Lilith's eyes must read as gold.
- The eye color should remain bright and readable at small sizes.
- Do not shift the eyes toward green, blue, white, red, orange, or brown.

## Hair

- Canon color: #0D0D0D
- Hair should read as black.
- Highlights, if used, must remain subtle and should not make the hair look gray, blue, purple, brown, or glossy-realistic.

## Lips

- Canon color: #000000
- Black lips are mandatory.
- Lips must remain visibly black in every expression.
- Do not use red, pink, purple, gray, or natural lip colors.

## Nails

- Canon color: #000000
- Nails must be black when visible.
- Do not use red, pink, gold, white, or natural nail colors for canon Lilith.

These colors carry Lilith's identity and should not be reinterpreted.

---

# Secondary Canon Colors

These colors support Lilith's full design and should remain consistent.

## Horns

- Canon color: #1A1A1A
- Horns should read as near-black.
- They may be slightly lighter than hair or clothing so the silhouette remains readable.
- Avoid white, bone, gray, red, gold, or purple horns unless explicitly requested as a non-canon variant.

## Clothing

- Canon color: #0D0D0D
- Clothing should read as black.
- Outfit accents may use canon gold.
- Avoid recoloring the outfit into red, purple, white, blue, or gray as a default.

## Jewelry and Gold Accents

- Canon color: #E0B22E
- Jewelry should read as warm gold.
- Use for earrings, necklace, and decorative gold accents.
- Avoid silver, bronze, rose-gold, copper, or white-metal replacements.

## Gems and Red Accents

- Canon color: #FF3B30
- Use only for small red gems, magical accents, or tiny decorative red details.
- This color should not replace the main skin color.

## Wings

- Outer wing structure: near-black / black, using #0D0D0D or #1A1A1A
- Inner wing membranes: #B71C1C
- Wings should remain black and red.
- Avoid feathered white wings, purple membranes, blue membranes, gray wings, or overly complex multicolor wings.

## Tail

- Canon color: #CC2828
- Tail shadow may use #B71C1C
- Tail should match the red skin family.
- Avoid black, purple, orange, or pink tails.

# Color Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy when deciding what must be preserved most strictly.

## Level 1 - Identity Colors

These cannot change in canon artwork:

1. Skin: #CC2828
2. Eyes: #FFC400
3. Hair: #0D0D0D
4. Lips: #000000
5. Nails: #000000

## Level 2 - Recognition Colors

These should remain consistent for visual recognition:

1. Horns: #1A1A1A
2. Clothing: #0D0D0D
3. Gold Jewelry: #E0B22E
4. Tail: #CC2828
5. Wing Membranes: #B71C1C

## Level 3 - Accent Colors

These may appear sparingly:

1. Gems: #FF3B30
2. Small magical red accents: #FF3B30
3. Minor shadow or cel-shading use: #B71C1C

Accent colors should never overpower the face.

# Deviation Rule

Lilith's canonical colors are locked.

Minor brightness variation is acceptable only for lighting, cel shading, compression, or format adaptation.

Allowed:

- Approximately ±10% brightness variation
- Same-hue shadowing
- Same-hue highlights when needed
- Minor anti-aliasing around edges
- Transparent background or neutral simple backgrounds

Not allowed:

- Hue shifts
- Alternate palettes
- Replacing black lips with red or pink lips
- Replacing gold eyes with another color
- Replacing gold jewelry with silver jewelry
- Making the skin pink, purple, orange, brown, gray, or human-toned
- Making black hair visibly blue, purple, brown, or gray
- Making the outfit colorful by default

Brightness changes should never alter the intended color identity.

---

# Shading Rules

Lilith should use simple mascot-vector coloring.

Preferred:

- Flat base colors
- One clean cel-shadow color
- Optional small highlight
- Minimal gradients
- Clear separation between shapes

Avoid:

- Painterly color blending
- Airbrush shading
- Complex gradients
- Realistic lighting
- Dramatic cinematic recoloring
- Colored rim lights that change her palette
- Heavy environmental color casts

The palette should remain recognizable even with simple cel shading.

---

# Lighting Rules

Lighting may support the artwork, but it must not redesign Lilith.

Allowed:

- Slightly lighter or darker versions of canon colors
- Simple cel shadows
- Soft neutral background lighting
- Small eye highlights
- Small jewelry highlights

Avoid:

- Strong blue, purple, green, or orange lighting that changes the character's colors
- Heavy glow effects that obscure the red-black-gold palette
- Cinematic color grading that makes the skin or outfit read as another color
- Lighting that makes the lips stop reading as black
- Lighting that makes the eyes stop reading as gold

If lighting changes the identity colors, reduce or remove the lighting.

---

# Line Art and Outline Color

Lilith should generally use black or near-black outlines.

Preferred outline colors:

- #000000
- #0D0D0D
- #1A1A1A

Use thick, clean outlines consistent with mascot-vector art.

Avoid:

- Colored outlines that weaken the silhouette
- Pale outlines
- Painterly edges
- Sketchy colored line art
- Low-contrast line art

The outline should help Lilith remain readable at small sizes.

## Black Shape Separation Rule

Lilith uses several near-black elements at the same time: hair, horns, clothing, nails, and line art.

Separate these forms through:

- Clean line weight
- Controlled near-black values
- Simple silhouette breaks
- Small canon-gold accents where appropriate
- Clear shape design

Do not separate black elements by shifting them into blue, purple, gray, brown, or another hue. Black elements should remain black or near-black.


---

# Background Color Rules

Backgrounds are not part of Lilith's canon body palette.

Preferred backgrounds:

- Transparent
- Plain white
- Simple neutral gray
- Simple dark neutral
- Minimal flat color shape
- Uncluttered backdrop

Avoid backgrounds that:

- Compete with her red skin
- Blend into her black hair or clothing
- Overpower the gold eyes
- Add too many new saturated colors
- Make the silhouette hard to read
- Create a painterly or realistic scene that clashes with the mascot style

For emotes, stickers, and icons, transparent backgrounds are preferred.

---

# Format-Specific Color Rules

## Discord Emotes and Small Icons

Prioritize:

- Bright gold eyes
- Clear black lips
- Strong red skin
- Strong black hair silhouette
- High contrast between face and hair

Avoid tiny color details that disappear at small size.

## Expression Sheets

Every expression must preserve:

- Gold eyes
- Black lips
- Visible fangs
- Red skin
- Black sidebang

Do not let emotional mouth shapes erase the black lip color.

## Pose Sheets

Maintain consistent colors across every pose on the sheet.

Do not allow one pose to have lighter skin, different eye color, missing lips, or different jewelry color.

## Cookbook Art

Keep Lilith's palette dominant.

Props such as cauldrons, books, ingredients, gems, bones, herbs, and bottles should not overpower the red-black-gold character read.

## Promotional Art

Promotional art may use stronger composition and lighting, but Lilith's identity colors must remain intact.

Do not use dramatic lighting as an excuse to change her palette.

---

# No-Wings / No-Tail Variant Color Rules

If wings or tail are removed:

- Do not change the face colors.
- Do not change the body colors.
- Do not change the outfit colors.
- Do not compensate by adding new accent colors.
- Do not make the silhouette or palette more generic.

A no-wing or no-tail Lilith still uses the same canon palette.

---

# Common Color Drift Problems

Reject or revise artwork if:

- Skin becomes pink, purple, orange, brown, gray, or pale
- Eyes are not gold
- Lips are not black
- Lips become too small to read
- Hair is not black
- Hair becomes blue, purple, gray, or brown
- Jewelry becomes silver or white metal
- Nails are missing or not black
- Clothing becomes colorful by default
- Horns become bone-white, gray, red, or gold
- Wings lose the black-and-red read
- Tail is not red
- Heavy lighting obscures the canon palette
- The overall palette feels like a different demoness character

Color drift should be treated as a design error, not a minor variation.

# Palette Use Summary

Use these rules as the fastest color check:

- Skin must be red.
- Eyes must be gold.
- Hair must be black.
- Lips must be black.
- Nails must be black.
- Clothing must be black.
- Jewelry must be gold.
- Wings, if present, must be black and red.
- Tail, if present, must be red.
- Shading must be simple and should not shift hue.

The character should always read as red, black, and gold.

---

# Final Color Approval Checklist

Before approving any Lilith artwork, check:

1. Is her skin clearly #CC2828 red or a close same-hue variant?
2. Are her eyes clearly #FFC400 gold?
3. Is her hair clearly black?
4. Are her lips clearly black and visible?
5. Are her nails black when visible?
6. Is her outfit primarily black?
7. Is her jewelry gold rather than silver or another metal?
8. Are her wings black and red when included?
9. Is her tail red when included?
10. Has the artwork avoided hue shifts and alternate palettes?
11. Does the palette still work at small emote size?
12. Does the overall character still read as canon Lilith?

If any answer is no, revise the colors.
