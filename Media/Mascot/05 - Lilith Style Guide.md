# LILITH STYLE GUIDE - CONSOLIDATED

## Purpose

This document defines how Lilith should be visually rendered.

The Canon Character Guide defines who Lilith is.
The Character Requirements define what must be present.
The Canon Color Palette defines exact colors.
The Body Proportion Guide defines detailed body proportion rules.
This Style Guide defines how Lilith should be drawn.

This file exists to prevent art style drift across:

- Emotes
- Pose sheets
- Expression sheets
- Character reference sheets
- Cookbook illustrations
- Icons
- Stickers
- Promotional artwork
- Mod artwork
- Item or UI-adjacent artwork

The Style Guide does not override the Face Reference, Character Reference, Canon Character Guide, Character Requirements, or Canon Color Palette.

When uncertain, preserve readability, simplicity, recognition, consistency, and mascot appeal.

---

# Core Style Identity

Lilith is a mascot-style chibi demoness.

Her target style is:

- Mascot art
- Vector-cartoon art
- Sticker art
- Discord emote art
- Clean character reference art
- Simplified gothic fantasy cartoon art

Clean chibi-cartoon influence is allowed when it remains simple, bold, mascot-like, and emote-readable. Avoid highly rendered anime styling that softens or complicates the character.

Lilith should not be rendered as:

- Realistic fantasy art
- Semi-realistic fantasy art
- Painterly illustration
- Highly rendered anime art
- Horror demon art
- Fashion illustration
- Complex concept art
- Soft airbrushed character art

Lilith should feel like a clean, reproducible mascot character, not a detailed painting.

---

# Style Philosophy

Every style decision should prioritize:

1. Readability
2. Simplicity
3. Recognition
4. Consistency
5. Reproducibility
6. Emote usability

These are more important than:

- Realism
- Rendering complexity
- Tiny detail
- Dramatic lighting
- Texture quality
- Painterly finish
- Anatomical complexity

If a detail does not improve recognition, simplify it.

If a rendering choice makes Lilith harder to read at small size, remove it.

---

# Shape Language

Lilith should be built from large, simple, readable shapes.

Required:

- Clear silhouette
- Large readable forms
- Compact chibi proportions
- Simplified anatomy
- Strong head shape
- Strong sidebang shape
- Simple horn shapes
- Simple wing shapes when wings are included
- Simple tail curve when tail is included
- Minimal visual clutter

Avoid:

- Tiny decorative shapes
- Complex textures
- Excessive ornamentation
- Overly thin limbs
- Overly sharp realistic anatomy
- Busy internal detail
- Silhouette-breaking clutter

Lilith should remain recognizable from her outline before internal details are added.

---

# Line Art

Line art is one of Lilith's most important style anchors.

Required:

- Thick outlines
- Clean outlines
- Smooth curves
- Consistent line weight
- Strong outer contour
- Readable silhouette
- Clear separation between major forms

Preferred:

- Bold outer contour
- Slightly thinner internal lines
- Smooth vector-like curves
- Rounded cartoon-friendly line joins
- Clean line intersections

Avoid:

- Sketchy lines
- Scratchy lines
- Thin delicate linework
- Heavy realistic inking
- Variable comic-book inking
- Messy crosshatching
- Painterly edge blending
- Lines that disappear at small size

Line art should make Lilith readable even before shading is added.

---

# Silhouette Readability

Lilith must remain recognizable from silhouette alone.

Silhouette anchors:

- Large chibi head
- Black horns
- Black sidebang
- Compact body
- Gothic outfit shape
- Bat wings when included
- Red spaded tail when included

Silhouette rules:

- Keep the head large and dominant.
- Keep the body compact.
- Keep limbs simple and readable.
- Do not let props obscure the head, lips, eyes, horns, or sidebang.
- Do not allow wings or tail to overwhelm the body.
- Do not let backgrounds merge with the character outline.

A strong Lilith image should still read clearly as Lilith in black silhouette.

---

# Detail Level

Lilith should be simple enough to work at small sizes.

She must remain readable at:

- 64x64
- 128x128
- Discord emote size
- Small icon size
- Sticker thumbnail size
- Reference sheet thumbnail size

Detail rules:

- Use fewer, larger details.
- Prioritize the face over costume detail.
- Prioritize expression over rendering.
- Prioritize silhouette over texture.
- Remove details that disappear at small size.
- Avoid decorative clutter that competes with the face.

The most important readable details are:

- Heavy-lidded gold eyes
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Black horns
- Red skin
- Compact chibi body

Tiny details are optional. Identity details are not.

---

# Facial Rendering

The face carries most of Lilith's identity and must be the clearest part of the image.

Required:

- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp gold eyes
- Strong eyebrow readability
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Slight smug or mischievous expression

Facial rendering priorities:

1. Eyes
2. Eyelids and brows
3. Black lips
4. Fangs
5. Sidebang silhouette
6. Nose and minor facial details

Avoid:

- Soft innocent eyes
- Large generic anime eyes
- Missing black lips
- Missing fangs
- Tiny unreadable mouth shapes
- Overly realistic facial rendering
- Excessively detailed noses
- Blushed or softened expressions that erase smugness

Eyes and mouth must remain readable before any other facial details.

---

# Eye Rendering

Lilith's eyes should stay sharp, gold, and heavy-lidded.

Required:

- Gold eyes
- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp eye shape
- Confident gaze
- Mischievous expression
- Clear eye direction

Avoid:

- Round innocent eyes
- Large watery eyes
- Soft helpless eyes
- Tiny dot eyes unless used in a simplified emote variant that still preserves Lilith's identity
- Hyper-detailed realistic eyes
- Excessive highlights that make the eyes too sparkly or innocent

The eyes should feel clever and amused, not naive.

---

# Mouth, Lip, and Fang Rendering

Black lips are mandatory and must stay readable.

Required:

- Black lips visible in every expression
- Small fangs visible whenever possible
- Clear mouth shape
- Expression readable at small size
- Mischievous or smug mouth language

Avoid:

- Red lips
- Pink lips
- Missing lip shape
- Mouths that erase the black lip silhouette
- Missing fangs
- Huge vampire fangs
- Realistic teeth detail
- Monster-like mouths

Open-mouth expressions should still preserve the black lip shape.

Closed-mouth expressions should still show small visible fangs when possible.

---

# Hair Rendering

Lilith's hair should read as a bold black shape first.

Required:

- Black hair
- Strong sidebang silhouette
- Large hair masses
- Minimal strand detail
- Clean cartoon shape language
- Readable hair outline

Avoid:

- Individual hair strands
- Excessive flyaways
- Realistic hair texture
- Painterly hair shine
- Complex layered locks
- Hair colors other than black
- Hairstyles that remove the sidebang identity

Hair should be drawn as a simplified silhouette, not a realistic texture.

---

# Horn Rendering

Horns should be simple and readable.

Required:

- Black or near-black horns
- Clean simple shape
- Symmetrical enough to read clearly
- Thick outline
- Strong silhouette

Avoid:

- Overly detailed horn ridges
- Bone-colored horns
- Huge horns that dominate the face
- Tiny horns that disappear at icon size
- Realistic horn texture

Horns should support the silhouette without stealing attention from the face.

---

# Wing Rendering

When included, wings should be simple bat wings.

Required:

- Black outer structure
- Red inner membranes
- Simple readable bat-wing shape
- Clean thick outlines
- Minimal internal detail
- Balanced scale behind the body

Avoid:

- Feathered wings
- Dragon wings with excessive detail
- Huge wings that dominate Lilith
- Complex membrane texture
- Realistic anatomy
- Wing details that clutter the silhouette

Wings are important recognition features, but the face remains more important.

---

# Tail Rendering

When included, the tail should be simple and readable.

Required:

- Red tail
- Spaded tip
- Clean curve
- Thick outline
- Simple shape
- Readable silhouette

Avoid:

- Overly long tail clutter
- Complex tail poses that distract from Lilith
- Wrong tail color
- Missing spade tip when the tail is included
- Realistic reptile tail texture

The tail should add personality and silhouette, not visual noise.

---

# Outfit Rendering

Lilith's outfit should remain gothic, black, simple, and readable.

Required:

- Black clothing
- Simple gothic dress or outfit shape
- Gold accents where appropriate
- Clean silhouette
- Minimal decorative clutter

Avoid:

- Excessive lace detail
- Complex armor rendering
- Realistic fabric folds
- Busy costume redesigns
- Fashion-doll detailing
- Outfit details that compete with the face

The outfit should support Lilith's identity, not become the focal point.

---

# Jewelry and Accent Rendering

Gold jewelry and accents should be simple and readable.

Required:

- Gold color identity
- Simple shapes
- Clean outlines
- Limited accent use
- Readable at small size when visible

Avoid:

- Silver replacing gold
- Overly ornate jewelry
- Tiny jewelry details that clutter the design
- Gem-heavy redesigns
- Jewelry that distracts from the face

Gold accents should support the red-black-gold palette.

---

# Hand and Nail Rendering

Hands should remain simplified and mascot-friendly.

Required:

- Five fingers when fingers are clearly visible
- Black nails
- Simple hand shapes
- Clear gesture readability

Avoid:

- Four-finger cartoon hands
- Missing black nails
- Realistic hand anatomy
- Overly long claws
- Tiny finger details that become messy

Hands should communicate the pose without stealing focus from the face.

---

# Shading Style

Lilith should use flat color or simple cel shading.

Maximum shading complexity:

- Base color
- One shadow tone
- Optional small highlight tone

Preferred:

- Flat base colors
- Clean cel shadows
- Hard-edged shadow shapes
- Minimal highlights
- Simple shadow placement

Avoid:

- Airbrush shading
- Soft gradients
- Painterly blending
- Realistic lighting
- Subsurface scattering
- Complex bounce light
- Heavy glow effects
- Detailed texture shading

Shading should clarify form, not show off rendering.

---

# Highlight Rules

Highlights should be minimal and controlled.

Acceptable:

- Small eye highlights
- Small jewelry highlights
- Minimal hair shine if it does not create texture clutter
- Simple cel-style clothing highlights when needed

Avoid:

- Glossy realistic skin highlights
- Excessive sparkles
- Painterly shine
- Complex rim lighting
- Highlights that shift the canon colors
- Highlights that make black hair look gray or blue

Highlights should not weaken the canon palette.

---

# Color Usage

Use the Canon Color Palette as the source of truth.

Required color behavior:

- Preserve red skin
- Preserve black hair
- Preserve black lips
- Preserve gold eyes
- Preserve gold jewelry
- Preserve black clothing
- Preserve black nails
- Preserve red tail when included
- Preserve black and red wings when included

Avoid:

- Hue shifts
- Alternate palettes
- Pink or purple skin
- Red or pink lips
- Silver jewelry replacing gold
- Blue or gray hair interpretations
- Lighting that changes identity colors

Minor lighting adjustment is acceptable only if Lilith still clearly reads as red, black, and gold.

---

# Texture Rules

Lilith should not rely on texture.

Preferred:

- Flat graphic shapes
- Clean color blocks
- Simple cel shadows
- Smooth outlines

Avoid:

- Skin texture
- Fabric texture
- Hair texture
- Horn texture
- Wing membrane texture
- Brush texture
- Grunge overlays
- Noise overlays

Texture usually pushes Lilith away from mascot style.

---

# Anatomy Style

Lilith uses compact mature-chibi mascot anatomy.

Required:

- Large head
- Small compact body
- Short legs
- Simplified limbs
- Slightly narrower face
- Readable gesture
- Stable, sturdy silhouette

Avoid:

- Realistic anatomy
- Long legs
- Long torso
- Thin fashion-body proportions
- Hyper-muscular anatomy
- Hypersexualized anatomy
- Detailed anatomical rendering

Anatomy should be expressive and simple, not realistic.

---

# Expression Style

Expressions may be exaggerated, but they must remain recognizably Lilith.

Required across expressions:

- Black visible lips
- Gold eyes
- Heavy upper eyelid influence whenever possible
- Visible fangs whenever possible
- Sidebang silhouette
- Mischievous personality read

Allowed:

- Exaggerated brows
- Open-mouth expressions
- Squinting
- Smirking
- Laughing
- Shocked or surprised variants
- Gremlin-like expressions

Avoid:

- Expressions that remove the black lips
- Expressions that remove fangs entirely
- Expressions that make her look innocent by default
- Expressions that make her cruel or grimdark
- Generic anime reaction faces that erase her identity

Even exaggerated expressions should keep Lilith's smug, clever edge.

---

# Pose Style

Poses should be clear, readable, and mascot-friendly.

Required:

- Strong silhouette
- Clear gesture
- Compact body proportions
- Face visible whenever possible
- Expression readable
- Hands simple and readable
- Props secondary

Avoid:

- Poses that stretch the body taller
- Poses that make limbs too long
- Poses that hide the face
- Poses that obscure lips, eyes, or fangs
- Overly complex action poses
- Realistic dynamic anatomy

Pose energy should feel playful, confident, and theatrical.

---

# Prop Style

Props should be simple and secondary.

Required:

- Props should support the theme.
- Props should not cover the face.
- Props should not hide the lips, eyes, fangs, or sidebang.
- Props should use simple shapes.
- Props should remain readable at small size.

Avoid:

- Oversized props dominating Lilith
- Too many props
- Realistic prop detail
- Complex item textures
- Props that confuse the silhouette
- Props that turn the image into a scene instead of a character image

If a prop competes with Lilith's face, simplify or shrink the prop.

---

# Background Style

Backgrounds should be minimal.

Preferred:

- Transparent background
- Plain solid background
- Simple shape backdrop
- Minimal decorative elements
- Light thematic framing only when needed

Avoid:

- Busy scenery
- Complex rooms
- Heavy atmospheric effects
- Detailed backgrounds
- Backgrounds that touch or obscure the silhouette
- Backgrounds that overpower Lilith

For emotes, icons, and stickers, use transparent or very simple backgrounds.

---

# Composition Rules

Lilith should always be the focal point.

General composition requirements:

- Character centered or clearly dominant
- Face visible
- Expression readable
- Silhouette clear
- Props secondary
- Background simple

For pose sheets:

- Consistent scale
- Neutral background
- Equal spacing
- Full pose readable
- No unnecessary labels beyond requested text

For expression sheets:

- Face dominant
- Expressions clearly separated
- Same character proportions across expressions
- Lips and fangs checked in every panel

For cookbook art:

- Waist-up composition preferred
- Face clearly visible
- Simple table or cauldron setup
- Limited object count
- Props do not obscure identity

For emotes:

- Face dominant
- Expression prioritized
- Minimal body detail
- Transparent background preferred
- Readable at 128x128

For icons:

- Simple silhouette
- Strong contrast
- Minimal details
- Canon colors preserved

# Format-Specific Style Rules

## Discord Emotes

Prioritize:

- Face
- Eyes
- Lips
- Fangs
- Expression
- Silhouette

Reduce:

- Outfit detail
- Jewelry detail
- Background detail
- Prop detail
- Wing and tail complexity

## Reference Sheets

Prioritize:

- Consistency
- Clean outlines
- Neutral lighting
- Reproducible shapes
- Accurate proportions
- Clear front, side, back, or 3/4 views when requested

Avoid:

- Dramatic lighting
- Stylized camera angles
- Pose exaggeration that changes proportions
- Background clutter

## Promotional Art

Promotional art may be slightly more polished, but must still preserve:

- Mascot-vector simplicity
- Canon palette
- Clean silhouette
- Strong face identity
- Limited rendering complexity

## Cookbook Art

Prioritize:

- Clear Lilith face
- Mischievous cooking energy
- Simple props
- Limited object count
- Clean readable composition

Avoid:

- Busy kitchen scenery
- Excessive ingredients
- Props covering her face
- Realistic food rendering

---

# No-Wings / No-Tail Style Rule

When wings or tail are removed, only those elements should be removed.

Do not change:

- Face
- Hair
- Sidebang
- Body proportions
- Outfit
- Colors
- Expression language
- Chibi mascot style
- Silhouette width unless required by the removed parts

Removing wings or tail must not make Lilith taller, thinner, softer, more realistic, or less recognizable.

---

# Complexity Control Rule

Lilith should be easy to redraw.

A successful Lilith design should be reproducible by another artist without needing complex rendering knowledge.

Use this rule:

- If the detail improves recognition, keep it.
- If the detail only adds decoration, simplify it.
- If the detail disappears at small size, remove it.
- If the detail competes with the face, remove it.
- If the detail makes Lilith harder to reproduce, simplify it.

The face, silhouette, palette, and expression matter more than decorative polish.

---

# Common Style Drift Problems

Reject or revise artwork if it becomes:

- Too realistic
- Too painterly
- Too airbrushed
- Too detailed
- Too texture-heavy
- Too anime-generic
- Too soft and innocent
- Too grimdark
- Too seductive
- Too horror-like
- Too tall or thin
- Too cluttered
- Too dependent on background effects
- Too dependent on lighting effects
- Too far from mascot-vector simplicity

Also reject or revise if:

- Lines are too thin
- Silhouette is unclear
- Face is not the focal point
- Lips are not readable
- Fangs are missing
- Eyes are not sharp or gold
- Hair loses the sidebang silhouette
- Colors drift from the canon palette

---

# Style Approval Checklist

Before approving Lilith artwork, check:

1. Does the artwork use mascot-vector cartoon styling?
2. Are the outlines thick, clean, and readable?
3. Is the silhouette clear?
4. Is the face the focal point?
5. Are the heavy-lidded gold eyes readable?
6. Are the black lips clearly visible?
7. Are small fangs visible where possible?
8. Is the sidebang silhouette preserved?
9. Are the shapes simple and easy to redraw?
10. Is the shading flat or simple cel shading?
11. Are textures, gradients, and painterly rendering avoided?
12. Are the canon colors preserved?
13. Does the image still read at 128x128?
14. Do props and background remain secondary?
15. Does Lilith still feel mischievous, clever, and pleased with herself?

If any answer is no, simplify or revise the artwork.

---

# Final Style Rule

Lilith's style should always support her identity.

She is not a realism test, a rendering showcase, or a complex fantasy illustration.

She is a clean, mischievous, mascot-style chibi demoness designed to be instantly recognizable, easy to reproduce, and readable at small sizes.

When in doubt, make her simpler, clearer, bolder, and more expressive.
