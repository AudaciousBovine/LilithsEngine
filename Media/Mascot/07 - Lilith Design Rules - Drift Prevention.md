# LILITH DESIGN RULES - DRIFT PREVENTION

## Purpose

This document defines the rules for identifying, preventing, and correcting Lilith design drift.

It exists to make sure every future Lilith artwork still feels like the same character, even when the pose, expression, activity, format, props, or composition changes.

The Canon Character Guide defines who Lilith is.  
The Character Requirements define what must be present.  
The Canon Color Palette defines exact colors.  
The Style Guide defines how Lilith should be rendered.  
The Body Proportion Guide defines detailed body proportion rules.  
This document defines what must be rejected or revised when artwork begins drifting away from canon.

Use this document when reviewing, correcting, regenerating, or approving Lilith artwork.

---

# Core Drift Rule

Lilith may change pose, expression, activity, props, framing, or format.

Lilith must not change identity.

A new artwork is drifting if it makes Lilith feel like a different character, even if many individual details are technically present.

The most important question is:

> Does this still immediately read as Lilith?

If the answer is not clearly yes, revise the artwork.

---

# Source Priority

When evaluating drift, use the project source priority order:

1. Lilith Face Reference
2. Lilith Character Reference
3. Project Master Instruction
4. Canon Character Guide
5. Character Requirements
6. Canon Color Palette
7. Style Guide
8. Body Proportion Guide
9. Expression Bible
10. Pose Bible
11. Variant-specific guides
12. Design Rules / Drift Prevention
13. Current prompt

Higher-priority sources override lower-priority sources.

The Face Reference is the highest authority for Lilith's identity. If a new artwork conflicts with the Face Reference, the new artwork is wrong.

The current prompt may request a pose, expression, prop, scene, or variant, but it must not erase Lilith's face identity, personality, palette, proportions, or mascot readability.

This document is primarily a review and enforcement layer. Use the positive design rules from the Canon Character Guide, Requirements, Palette, Style Guide, Body Proportion Guide, Expression Bible, and Pose Bible first, then use this document to reject or correct results that drift away from canon.

---

# What Counts As Design Drift

Design drift occurs when artwork moves away from canon Lilith in one or more of these areas:

- Face identity
- Expression language
- Personality read
- Body proportions
- Silhouette
- Color palette
- Outfit identity
- Wing or tail design
- Hand and nail details
- Rendering style
- Detail level
- Pose behavior
- Prop dominance
- Background complexity
- Variant interpretation

Small errors can become major drift when they affect recognition.

For example, missing black lips are not a small mouth-detail error. They remove one of Lilith's strongest identity features.

---

# The Lilith Recognition Test

Before accepting any artwork, check whether Lilith still communicates the following read:

> Mischievous, clever, smug, amused, and pleased with herself.

The correct read is not simply:

- Cute
- Pretty
- Evil
- Angry
- Sad
- Generic demon
- Generic chibi girl
- Generic fantasy villain

The correct read is:

- Sassy
- Mischievous
- Clever
- Confident
- Self-assured
- Playfully villainous
- Trickster-like
- Amused by herself

If the artwork loses this emotional identity, revise it.

---

# Highest-Risk Drift Areas

The most common and most serious Lilith drift problems are:

1. Missing or unclear black lips
2. Missing or unclear fangs
3. Losing the heavy-lidded gold eyes
4. Losing the smug or mischievous expression
5. Losing the black sidebang silhouette
6. Making the body too tall
7. Making the body too thin
8. Making the style too painterly or realistic
9. Making the palette shift away from red, black, and gold
10. Making Lilith read as a generic demoness instead of herself

These are priority revision triggers.

---

# Face Drift

Lilith's face carries most of her identity.

Artwork is drifting if the face loses:

- Heavy upper eyelids
- Sharp gold eyes
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Slight smug expression
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Confident gaze
- Mischievous amusement

The face must remain recognizable even if the body, pose, outfit detail, or props are simplified.

## Reject Face Drift If

- Eyes become large, round, innocent, or generic
- Eyelids disappear or open too wide by default
- Gold eyes become another color
- Lips become red, pink, natural, gray, or missing
- Fangs disappear in a face-focused image
- Sidebang shape disappears
- Eyebrows lose expression readability
- The mouth shape removes the black lip silhouette
- The expression becomes blank, sad, helpless, or randomly angry
- The face reads as a different demon girl

## Correction Priority

When correcting face drift, preserve these first:

1. Heavy-lidded gold eyes
2. Black visible lips
3. Small visible fangs
4. Smug mischievous expression
5. Black sidebang silhouette

These five features are more important than most outfit, wing, tail, prop, or background details.

---

# Eye Drift

Lilith's eyes should feel sharp, gold, amused, and knowing.

Artwork is drifting if the eyes become:

- Too round
- Too innocent
- Too wide by default
- Too soft
- Too blank
- Too angry without mischief
- A color other than gold
- Hidden by effects, props, or hair

Lilith's eyes should not feel helpless, surprised by default, or generically cute.

Even in surprised or shocked expressions, her eyes should still preserve enough of her identity to read as Lilith.

---

# Mouth, Lip, and Fang Drift

Black lips are mandatory.

Small visible fangs are mandatory in most expressions, especially face-focused artwork, emotes, expression sheets, icons, and close-up views.

Artwork is drifting if:

- Lips are missing
- Lips are too small to read
- Lips are red, pink, purple, gray, or natural-colored
- Open-mouth expressions erase the black lip shape
- Fangs are missing without a clear expression reason
- Fangs become huge vampire fangs
- Teeth become monster-like or horror-like
- The mouth becomes generic anime-style without Lilith's lip identity

If the expression requires an open mouth, the black lip outline or lip shape must still remain readable.

---

# Expression Drift

Lilith may show many emotions, but every expression must still feel like Lilith.

Required expression undertone:

- Mischievous
- Amused
- Clever
- Confident
- Knowing
- Slightly smug when appropriate

Expressions can become more extreme, but they should not erase the core character.

## Reject Expression Drift If

- Happy becomes innocent and generic
- Angry becomes pure rage without cleverness
- Sad becomes depressed or helpless
- Surprised loses all Lilith facial structure
- Laughing loses black lips or fangs
- Proud loses smugness
- Approving loses fangs or mouth identity
- Confused becomes blank rather than suspicious or curious
- Unimpressed becomes lifeless rather than dry and judgmental

When in doubt, add back the amused, knowing quality.

---

# Personality Drift

Lilith is a playful villain and trickster, not a cruel monster or generic demoness.

Artwork is drifting if Lilith reads as:

- Cruel
- Grimdark
- Depressed
- Helpless
- Innocent by default
- Randomly angry
- Generic fantasy demon
- Generic cute mascot
- Realistic fantasy woman
- Seductive archetype instead of mischievous mascot

Her mischief should feel playful, not brutal.

Her confidence should feel self-satisfied, not cold or lifeless.

Her villainy should feel theatrical and amusing, not frightening or cruel.

---

# Body Proportion Drift

Lilith uses compact mature-chibi mascot proportions.

Artwork is drifting if her body becomes:

- Too tall
- Too thin
- Too long-legged
- Too long-torsoed
- Too realistic
- Too fashion-doll-like
- Too anatomically mature
- Too narrow after wings or tail are removed
- Too stretched by the pose

Required body read:

- Large head
- Short compact body
- Short legs
- Small torso
- Simplified limbs
- Wide or sturdy silhouette
- Mascot-friendly proportions

Removing wings or tail must not make Lilith taller, narrower, thinner, or less chibi.

If the pose requires motion, bend the compact body; do not stretch the body into realistic proportions.

---

# Silhouette Drift

Lilith should be recognizable from silhouette or thumbnail read.

Core silhouette markers:

- Large head
- Compact body
- Black horns
- Black sidebang
- Gothic black outfit mass
- Bat wings when included
- Red spaded tail when included

Artwork is drifting if:

- Horns become unclear or disappear
- Sidebang disappears
- Body becomes a thin vertical shape
- Outfit silhouette becomes generic
- Wings become too complex or dominate the silhouette
- Tail becomes unclear or non-spaded
- Props cover the head or face
- Hair shape changes enough to look like a different character

If the silhouette is unclear, simplify it.

---

# Color Drift

Lilith's color identity is red, black, and gold.

Required canon colors:

| Element | Canon Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Skin | #CC2828 |
| Skin Shadow | #B71C1C |
| Hair | #0D0D0D |
| Horns | #1A1A1A |
| Clothing | #0D0D0D |
| Lips | #000000 |
| Nails | #000000 |
| Eyes | #FFC400 |
| Gold Jewelry | #E0B22E |
| Gems | #FF3B30 |
| Wing Membranes | #B71C1C |
| Tail | #CC2828 |

Artwork is drifting if:

- Skin becomes pink, purple, orange, brown, gray, or pale
- Eyes are not gold
- Hair is not black
- Lips are not black
- Nails are not black when visible
- Jewelry becomes silver or white metal
- Clothing becomes colorful by default
- Wings lose the black-and-red read
- Tail is not red
- Lighting changes the identity colors
- The overall palette no longer reads red, black, and gold

Minor brightness variation is acceptable only when the hue identity remains intact.

Hue shifts are not acceptable for canon Lilith.

---

# Style Drift

Lilith should be rendered as clean mascot-vector cartoon art.

Required style read:

- Thick clean outlines
- Simple shapes
- Clear silhouette
- Minimal detail
- Flat color or simple cel shading
- Emote-friendly readability
- Easy-to-redraw design

Artwork is drifting if it becomes:

- Realistic
- Painterly
- Airbrushed
- Semi-realistic fantasy art
- Over-rendered anime art
- Texture-heavy
- Lighting-heavy
- Too detailed for emotes
- Too complex to redraw consistently

Rendering quality should never override character clarity.

If a detail does not improve recognition, simplify it.

---

# Detail Drift

Lilith is intentionally simple.

Artwork is drifting if it adds too much:

- Hair strand detail
- Clothing trim
- Jewelry complexity
- Wing membrane detail
- Background decoration
- Texture
- Lighting effects
- Tiny props
- Sparkles, particles, or magic effects

Details are only acceptable if they preserve readability and do not compete with the face.

The face must always remain the focal point.

---

# Outfit Drift

Lilith's outfit should remain gothic, black, simple, and recognizable.

Required outfit identity:

- Black gothic dress or black gothic outfit
- Gold accents
- Gold jewelry
- Black stockings or dark legwear when visible
- Black boots or dark footwear when visible

Artwork is drifting if:

- The outfit becomes unrelated to Lilith
- The outfit becomes too modern by default
- The outfit becomes colorful without request
- The outfit becomes overly armored or complex
- Tiny outfit details overpower the face
- The outfit changes the character into a different archetype

Outfit variants are allowed only when explicitly requested and must preserve face identity, palette, proportions, and mischievous personality.

---

# Jewelry and Accent Drift

Lilith's jewelry should remain warm gold and simple.

Artwork is drifting if:

- Gold becomes silver, white, copper, or rose-gold
- Jewelry becomes overly complex
- Earrings disappear in clear ear views without reason
- Necklace or central gold accents disappear in upper-body views without reason
- Accessories cover the eyes, lips, fangs, or sidebang
- Random new accessories start feeling like canon

Jewelry supports recognition but must not dominate the design.

---

# Wing Drift

Canon Lilith includes bat wings unless a no-wing variant is requested.

Required wing identity:

- Bat-like
- Black outer structure
- Red inner membranes
- Simple readable shape
- Supports the silhouette without overpowering the body

Artwork is drifting if wings become:

- Feathered
- Angelic
- Overly complex dragon wings
- Too large for the composition
- A different color scheme
- Hidden or missing without request
- Obscuring the face

If wings are removed by request, only remove the wings. Do not redesign Lilith.

---

# Tail Drift

Canon Lilith includes a red spaded tail unless a no-tail variant is requested.

Required tail identity:

- Red
- Simple curved shape
- Spaded tip
- Readable silhouette

Artwork is drifting if the tail becomes:

- Missing without request
- Forked
- Furry
- Black, purple, orange, or pink
- Too complex
- Too dominant
- Lacking the spaded tip

If the tail is removed by request, only remove the tail. Do not redesign Lilith.

---

# Hand and Nail Drift

When hands are visible, they should support Lilith's pose and expression without becoming distracting.

Required:

- Five fingers when clearly visible
- Black nails
- Simplified mascot-style hands

Artwork is drifting if:

- Hands have four fingers by default
- Nails are missing or not black
- Hands become realistic and over-detailed
- Fingers become long horror claws
- Hands cover the face identity
- Gestures require realistic anatomy that changes the body style

Hands should be expressive but simple.

---

# Pose Drift

A pose may be dynamic, but it must not redesign Lilith.

Artwork is drifting if the pose:

- Makes her taller
- Makes her thinner
- Lengthens the legs
- Lengthens the torso
- Hides the face unnecessarily
- Covers the black lips, fangs, eyes, or sidebang
- Requires realistic anatomy
- Makes props more important than the character
- Breaks the compact mascot silhouette

The pose should serve Lilith. Lilith should not be redesigned to serve the pose.

---

# Prop Drift

Props are secondary.

Artwork is drifting if props:

- Cover the face
- Cover the eyes, lips, fangs, or sidebang
- Become larger or more detailed than Lilith
- Add too much visual clutter
- Introduce colors that overpower the red-black-gold palette
- Make the composition hard to read at small size
- Change the character's silhouette too much

When in doubt, simplify or remove prop detail before simplifying Lilith.

---

# Background Drift

Backgrounds should support the character, not compete with her.

Artwork is drifting if backgrounds:

- Become too busy
- Add too much scenery
- Add too many colors
- Obscure the silhouette
- Compete with the face
- Make the style feel painterly or realistic
- Reduce emote or sticker readability

Preferred backgrounds:

- Transparent
- Plain white
- Simple neutral gray
- Simple flat color shape
- Minimal thematic elements

Lilith should always remain the focal point.

---

# Variant Drift

Variants are allowed only when they preserve Lilith's identity.

A variant may change:

- Pose
- Expression
- Activity
- Prop
- Background
- Format
- Wing presence, if requested
- Tail presence, if requested
- Outfit theme, if explicitly requested

A variant must not change:

- Face identity
- Red skin
- Gold eyes
- Black hair
- Black sidebang
- Black lips
- Small fangs
- Heavy-lidded mischievous gaze
- Compact chibi proportions
- Red-black-gold palette
- Mischievous personality read

No-wing Lilith and no-tail Lilith are still Lilith. They are not new character designs.

---

# Format-Specific Drift Rules

## Emotes and Icons

Reject if:

- Face is not dominant
- Expression is unclear
- Lips are not black and readable
- Fangs disappear without reason
- Eyes are not gold and sharp
- Details vanish at 128x128
- Background competes with the face

## Expression Sheets

Reject if:

- Head shape changes between expressions
- Eye style changes between expressions
- Black lips disappear in any expression
- Fangs disappear in most expressions
- Sidebang changes shape
- One expression feels like a different character

## Pose Sheets

Reject if:

- Body proportions change between poses
- One pose becomes taller or thinner
- Face is hidden in multiple poses
- Props dominate the sheet
- Wings or tail change design between poses without reason

## Reference Sheets

Reject if:

- Views do not match each other
- Proportions change between views
- Colors change between views
- Face identity weakens in side or three-quarter views
- Back view implies a different outfit, hair, wing, or tail design

## Cookbook or Scene Art

Reject if:

- Scene clutter overpowers Lilith
- Props cover the face
- Expression is not mischievous or amused
- Background becomes more important than the character
- Palette becomes dominated by prop or environment colors

## Promotional Art

Reject if:

- Lighting changes Lilith's palette
- Rendering becomes too realistic or painterly
- Character identity is sacrificed for dramatic effects
- Face readability is reduced

---

# Automatic Revision Triggers

Revise the artwork if any of these occur:

- Black lips are missing
- Lips are not clearly black
- Fangs are missing in face-focused artwork
- Gold eyes are missing or changed
- Heavy eyelids are missing from the default expression
- Sidebang silhouette is missing
- Expression is not mischievous, smug, amused, or self-assured
- Body is too tall
- Body is too thin
- Legs are too long
- Torso is too long
- Skin shifts away from canon red
- Hair is not black
- Jewelry is not gold
- Nails are missing or not black when visible
- Horns are missing or unclear
- Wings are feathered or wrong-colored
- Tail lacks a spaded tip when included
- Style becomes painterly, realistic, or over-rendered
- Detail level harms icon or emote readability
- Props cover the face
- Background competes with Lilith
- Lilith reads as generic, sad, innocent, cruel, or seductive rather than mischievous

These are not minor mistakes. They weaken character identity.

---

# Correction Order

When fixing drift, correct in this order:

1. Face identity
2. Black lips and fangs
3. Heavy-lidded gold eyes
4. Sidebang silhouette
5. Mischievous expression
6. Body proportions
7. Color palette
8. Silhouette clarity
9. Horns, wings, tail, hands, and nails
10. Outfit and jewelry details
11. Style simplification
12. Props and background

Never improve props, background, or rendering at the cost of Lilith's face identity.

---

# Final Drift Approval Checklist

Before approving artwork as canon Lilith, check:

1. Does she immediately read as Lilith?
2. Are the heavy-lidded gold eyes present?
3. Are the black lips clearly visible?
4. Are small fangs visible where appropriate?
5. Is the black sidebang silhouette preserved?
6. Does she look mischievous, smug, amused, or self-assured?
7. Is the body compact mature-chibi rather than tall or thin?
8. Are the canon red, black, and gold colors preserved?
9. Are the horns present and readable?
10. Are black nails present when hands are visible?
11. Are wings bat-like, black-and-red, and simple when included?
12. Is the tail red with a spaded tip when included?
13. Is the style clean, simple, mascot-like, and emote-friendly?
14. Are props secondary to the character?
15. Is the background simple enough not to compete?
16. Would the artwork still read clearly at 128x128?
17. Could another artist redraw her easily?
18. Has the artwork avoided generic demoness drift?

If any core answer is no, revise the artwork before accepting it as canon Lilith.

---

# Final Rule

Drift prevention is not about making every image identical.

Lilith can be expressive, active, theatrical, funny, magical, dramatic, or playful.

But every version must still preserve the same character identity:

- Heavy-lidded gold eyes
- Black visible lips
- Small visible fangs
- Black sidebang silhouette
- Red skin
- Black hair
- Compact chibi mascot proportions
- Mischievous amused personality
- Simple red-black-gold palette
- Clean mascot-vector readability

If the artwork stops feeling like Lilith, revise it.
